# BUG-012: axi_read_engine issues ARs against SRAM space it has already taken

**Priority:** High -- same defect as rapids BUG-004; `axi_read_engine.sv` and
`axi_read_engine_beats.sv` are one file modulo the header.
**Status:** CLOSED 2026-09-27 (closing note at the end); was OPEN 2026-09-27. Found through the rapids copy's first direct test
(`test_axi_read_engine_beats`); STREAM has no engine-level test of its own, its
coverage of this block is the sram_controller and data-path tests.

## Mechanism

`axi_rd_alloc_space_free` reaches the engine three cycles after an AR handshake
(registered `r_alloc_req`, `stream_alloc_ctrl` count, `sram_controller`'s
boundary flop), and `w_space_ok` compared the next burst against that stale
view; with PIPELINE=1 a channel over-committed the SRAM by up to two bursts.
Independently, `m_axi_arvalid` was gated only by live `sched_rd_valid`, so a
stale round-robin grant the cycle after a channel's AR re-issued it while the
first was outstanding at PIPELINE=0. The write engine had both guards
(`w_pending_drain`); the read engine had neither. Full write-up: rapids BUG-004.

## Fix

Identical to the rapids fix: in-flight allocation pipeline
(`r_alloc_tminus1/2` subtracted from the registered view), `m_axi_arvalid` and
the stale-grant release keyed on the live `w_arb_request` term.
`formal/stream/axi_read_engine/axi_read_engine_flat.v` regenerated.

## Verification

See the closing note.

**CLOSED 2026-09-27.** `axi_read_engine.sv` carries the same fix as rapids BUG-004;
`formal/stream/axi_read_engine/axi_read_engine_flat.v` regenerated (its Makefile lacked the
monitor packages and could not regenerate at all -- fixed). The fix changed one thing in
STREAM's own suite: `test_datapath_rd` (varying lengths, PIPELINE=0) failed with 14 extra
beats. A/B on the same seed -- old engine passes, fixed engine fails -- and the AR trace
showed the old engine issuing AR pairs 30 ns apart in a "nopipe" build (the bug) while the
fixed engine's sequence is identical and serialized. The extra beats came from
`datapath_rd_test_tb.py`: it drained on `data_avail` and sampled after the popping edge,
which only works while the SRAM never runs dry. It now waits for the presented beat
(`axi_wr_sram_valid` and `axi_wr_sram_valid_comb`, the latter newly exposed by the DV wrapper
`datapath_rd_test.sv`) and samples before the pop. STREAM full regression: 680 passed, 3 skipped,
every area OK.
