# BUG-004: the read engine issues ARs against SRAM space it has already taken

**Priority:** High -- over-commits the source SRAM by up to two bursts whenever a
channel may hold more than one AR (PIPELINE=1), and at PIPELINE=0 lets a channel
issue a second AR while its first is outstanding.
**Status:** CLOSED 2026-09-27 (closing note at the end); was OPEN 2026-09-27. Found the day the engine got its first direct test
(rapids TASK-010 part B, `dv/tests/fub_beats/test_axi_read_engine_beats.py`).

## Symptom

`test_axi_read_engine_beats` at FUNC, 6 of 16 cases:

```
all  8ch 512b PIPELINE=1 x15 gaxi_backpressure : ch4: alloc 13 beats with only 0 free
starved 4ch 256b PIPELINE=0 x7                 : ch3: alloc 8 beats with only 7 free   (x10)
starved 8ch 512b PIPELINE=1 x15                : ch7: alloc 16 beats with only 15 free (x4)
```

The TB's SRAM-space model debits on every `axi_rd_alloc_req` pulse and credits as
the fill-side slave takes beats; the engine allocated more than the model had.
Data still arrived intact -- the test's memory is unbounded -- which is why no
system test ever tripped on it: STREAM's `sram_controller` only refuses a fill
write when its FIFO is actually full, and the data paths run at PIPELINE=0 with
eight channels sharing the round-robin, where the re-grant that triggers the
PIPELINE=0 form is rare.

## Mechanism (two forms, one gate)

`axi_rd_alloc_space_free` reaches the engine **three cycles** after an AR
handshake: `r_alloc_req` is registered (T+1), `stream_alloc_ctrl` counts it at
the next edge (T+2), and `sram_controller` flops the per-channel vector at its
boundary for 100 MHz closure (T+3). The write engine already carries a
`w_pending_drain` pipeline for exactly this staleness on `axi_wr_drain_data_avail`;
the read engine never got the twin, so `w_space_ok` compared the transfer size
against a view that still held the previous AR's beats.

The second form is the arbiter grant: `w_arb_request` is registered
(`r_arb_request`, added for timing), and `m_axi_arvalid` was gated only by live
`sched_rd_valid[grant]`. When a channel's AR handshakes at T and the round-robin
lands on it again at T+1 (the only eligible channel, as in the starved case),
`r_outstanding_limit` (set at the edge ending T, live at T+1) was not consulted,
so the second AR fired at PIPELINE=0 with the first outstanding.

## Fix

`axi_read_engine_beats.sv`:

1. `w_alloc_t` / `r_alloc_tminus1` / `r_alloc_tminus2`: sizes of the ARs that
   handshook in the last two cycles, subtracted from the registered view
   (`w_effective_space`) before `w_space_ok`. Two registered terms only -- a
   combinational "this cycle" term would feed `m_axi_arvalid` back into its own
   gate.
2. `m_axi_arvalid = w_arb_grant_valid && w_arb_request[w_arb_grant_id]` -- the
   live request term (scheduler valid, space net of in-flight, below the
   outstanding limit) instead of `sched_rd_valid` alone.
3. `w_stale_grant = w_arb_grant & ~w_arb_request`, so a grant the live term
   masks is released and the arbiter moves on.

The TB now also delays its reported space by the chain's two cycles
(`TEST_REPORT_DELAY`, default 2) so the engine is tested against the staleness
it meets in `src_data_path_beats`, not a friendlier one.

STREAM's `axi_read_engine.sv` is the same file modulo the header (the beats
engine is a copy of it); it carries the same defect -- stream BUG-012.

**CLOSED 2026-09-27.** Fix in `axi_read_engine_beats.sv`: in-flight allocation pipeline
(`r_alloc_tminus1/2`) netted from the registered space view; `m_axi_arvalid` and the
stale-grant release keyed on the live `w_arb_request`. Proof the test catches it: with the
pre-fix RTL swapped back, `test_axi_read_engine_beats` reports "ch2 already has 1 burst(s)
outstanding (limit 1)" and "alloc 8 beats with only 7 free"; with the fix FUNC 16/16 and FULL
across 4/8 channels, PIPELINE 0/1, 8/16/32-beat bursts and five consumer profiles. The
STREAM twin (stream BUG-012) exposed two testbenches that had been leaning on the double
issue to keep the SRAM ahead of their drain loops (STREAM `datapath_rd_test_tb`, rapids
`rapids_core_beats_tb`); both fixed, both documented in stream BUG-012 / rapids TASK-003.
