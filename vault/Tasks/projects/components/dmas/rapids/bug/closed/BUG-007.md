# BUG-007: a kick that ends in RD_ERROR wedges the channel's APB path through channel reset

**Priority:** High -- the HAS recovery flow for a descriptor error is "fix the
descriptor, reset the channel, restart"; after this defect the restart never
gets in.
**Status:** CLOSED 2026-09-27 (closing note at the end); was OPEN 2026-09-27.
Found by the recovery half of `test_address_range_validation` written for
BUG-006: after the refused kick and a channel reset, an in-range kick timed
out waiting for `apb_ready`.

## Mechanism

`r_apb_ip` ("APB transaction in progress") is set when the APB skid accepts a
kick and cleared only on the rising edge of `channel_idle`, i.e. when the
scheduler finishes the work that kick started. A kick that ends in `RD_ERROR`
(range refusal, AXI SLVERR/DECERR) never delivers a descriptor, so the
scheduler never leaves idle and the edge never comes. `apb_ready` requires
`!r_apb_ip`, and channel reset did not touch the flag, so the channel stayed
deaf to kicks until a hard reset. Same in STREAM (stream BUG-015).

## Fix

`rtl/fub_beats/descriptor_engine_beats.sv`: `r_channel_reset_active` clears
`r_apb_ip` (priority clause in its always block). The error-to-reset-to-kick
sequence is now the recovery half of the range test. Formal flat regenerated.
