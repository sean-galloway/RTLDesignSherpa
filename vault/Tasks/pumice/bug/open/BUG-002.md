# BUG-002: close-page modes reach only 62.8% of their own command-bus ceiling

**Status:** open 2026-09-24  **Priority:** P2 — bounds what close-page paging
can ever be worth on silicon; the board ships open page, so not a shipping
regression

Re-filed as a BUG on Sean's call 2026-09-24. It was tracked as [[ISSUE-002]]
and closed there with the residual ACCEPTED, on the grounds that the remaining
cost is pick-pipeline and bank-timer flop stages which Sean had ruled by design
for [[ISSUE-001]]. Sean's position now: a measured shortfall against a computed
ceiling is a DEFECT, whatever its cause, and belongs in the bug lane where it
will be seen. ISSUE-001 itself is dropped.

Per the convention an issue that turns out to be a defect is closed as an issue
and re-filed as a bug with each naming the other, so the trail is
[[ISSUE-002]] (closed, carries the full investigation) -> this.

## Expected vs observed

The arbiter issues at most ONE DFI command per cycle, so no mode can exceed
`BL_WORDS / commands_per_access` beats per cycle. Board geometry, 8-bank
rotation, refresh parked:

| mode | cmds/access | expected (ceiling) | observed | of ceiling |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| build_default / fixed_open | 1.04 | 96.0% | 98.97% | at it |
| static_open / adapt_time | 1.08 | 92.3% | 95.05% | at it |
| adapt_access | 1.09 | 91.9% | 92.75% | at it |
| **static_close / rbl_static** | **2.04** | **49.0%** | **30.77%** | **62.8%** |
| **rbl_dyn** | **1.64** | **61.0%** | **41.56%** | **68.1%** |

Every open-page mode sits at its ceiling. The close-page family does not.

## Root cause, established

From the DFI command trace: `ACT@130 ACT@136 ACT@140 ... WR@170` -- the first
column lands **8 aclk after its ACT** while tRCD is 3. The ~5-cycle excess is
the pick pipeline plus bank-timer registration on the ACT->column path, and
under CLOSE page every access pays it. During that window both candidate
classes are empty, so there is nothing for the pipeline to carry.

Ruled out by measurement, do not re-try: tRRD (30.77% at 1, 2 AND 4), tRCD/tRP/
tRC (37.5% with all three at minimum), tFAW, CAM depth (8 -> 16 gives
30.77 -> 32.43), the bank guards, bank recovery, and the outstanding dial
(board: static_close flat at 33.9 MB/s across OS 8/16/32 -- not latency-bound,
not outstanding-bound).

One partial fix already landed: removing the classify-time ACT mask's global
tFAW/tRRD gate took rbl_dyn 32.32% -> 41.56% (+29%). static_close did not move,
because its stall window has no columns available to carry.

## What fixing it would require

Shortening the ACT->column path -- i.e. removing a pick-pipeline stage or the
bank-timer registration stage. That is the trade this design makes on purpose
(flop stages for timing closure on a part at +0.016 ns WNS), which is why it
was accepted before. Filing it as a bug does not change the engineering; it
changes where the cost is recorded, so it is not mistaken for "characterized
and fine".

## Guard in place

`perf_paging_sweep` asserts a floor at 0.55 x ceiling and REPORTS the ratio
either way, so a step change below the measured point fails. Mutation-proven
2026-09-24: tightening the floor to 0.95 fails with the per-mode gap in the
message. That guard stays whatever happens to this bug -- it catches regression,
not the accepted shortfall.

**Do not re-open the 63% chase without a new idea.** The measured dead ends are
listed above; the only untried lever is pipeline depth, which is the design
point.


## 2026-09-24 — FIX ATTEMPTED: speculative column behind its ACT. Made it WORSE.

The obvious fix short of removing a pipeline stage, tried and measured. It does
not work, and the reason is architectural rather than a detail -- so this rules
out the whole approach rather than one implementation of it.

**The idea.** A column is blocked from even ENTERING the pipeline while its
bank's ACT is in flight, so under close page it only starts its own 3-4 stage
journey after the ACT fires and tRCD expires. Instead: treat the bank/row an
in-flight ACT is about to open as a PROSPECTIVE hit, let the column ride down
the pipeline behind its own ACT, and re-check the bank LIVE at the fire stage.
Exactly the shape of the fix that removed the classify-time tFAW/tRRD gate and
kept `w_act_gate_live` (+29% on rbl_dyn): classify optimistically, fire
authoritatively.

**Implemented** as `w_pend_act` / `w_pend_row` (from `rd_act_f`/`wr_act_f` plus
the selection-cycle `w_sel_*_act_f`), a prospective arm on `rhit`/`whit`, the
same arm on the `r_bank_rdwr_ready` term, a narrow relaxation of
`w_preact_bank_guard` for ONLY the column whose row that ACT opens, a live
`bank_rdwr_ready_i` gate on both column fire branches, and the same live gate
qualifying `w_c_col` so an unfirable column could not win the class vote.
Lints clean.

**Measured (board geometry, same sweep):**

| mode | before | after |
|---|---|---|
| static_close / rbl_static | 30.77% | **28.57%** |
| rbl_dyn | 41.56% | 41.29% |

**Worse, not better.** Reverted; baseline reproduces at 30.77 / 41.56 exactly.

**Why, and this is the part worth keeping.** The pre-pick stage is IN-ORDER: a
column that has been selected occupies the slot until it fires. Qualifying
`w_c_col` with the live bank gate stopped an unfirable column winning the CLASS
VOTE, but not consuming the SLOT -- so the speculative column sits in the
pipeline waiting for tRCD while blocking the ACT behind it. The optimism costs
more than the serialisation it removes.

**So the ACT->column serialisation is not a conservative gate that can be
relaxed. It is a consequence of an in-order pick pipeline**, and removing it
needs either out-of-order issue at the pre-pick stage (a rearchitecture) or
fewer stages (PUMICE-017/018 added them deliberately for timing, and this block
has already produced two silicon double-issue bugs from registered-feedback
latency).

**Do not retry the speculative-column approach.** Any variant hits the same
in-order slot occupancy. The remaining levers are the two above, and both are
large.
