# BUG-003: the fill unit test's capture-hold check counted the beats of a slot the hold does not cover

> Source: the jet-prime full regression of 2026-11-24 (run for BUG-002), seed
> 13525, `test_jet_prime_fill[8-9-full]`; fails on that seed at HEAD with no
> source changed.

**Priority:** P3 -- a test defect: a false failure under one seed in the
out-of-order responder; the RTL is correct.
**Status:** CLOSED 2026-11-24 -- check split per slot.
**Owner:** TBD
**Where:** `dv/tbclasses/jet_prime_fill_tb.py`, `_scen_capture_hold`.

## What was wrong

The scenario holds the capture of (set 5, way 1) and launches a second fill
to (set 9, way 0) behind it. Its own docstring says the hold is per
(set, way): out of order (func/full) the second fill may return first and
complete under the hold. The check after 60 cycles summed the beats of BOTH
slots and expected 0, so a second burst caught mid-stream at the sample
failed it. Seed 13525: slot 1 (held) 0 beats, slot 2 2 beats, out of order.

## Fix

Two checks: the held slot presents no beat (always); the slot behind it
presents none only with the in-order responder. Seed 13525 passes; the held
slot's check still fails if the hold leaks a beat.
