# BUG-005: a write-back victim passed on by a snoop still drains (jet carries jet-prime BUG-002)

> Source: jet-prime BUG-002 (2026-11-24). jet-prime is jet forked at
> 2be919254, and the defect is jet's: the rule is in jet's MAS ch02/03 and in
> `jet_wb_buffer`, `jet_control` and `jet_pair_fabric` unchanged.

## What is wrong

As jet-prime BUG-002: a WB entry answers a snoop at Modified, every such snoop
passes the line on (DT + PassDirty, or MakeInvalid), and the entry still
drains. If the new owner's write-back of the line lands first, the old drain
overwrites it. The entry also stays a snoop source until its BRESP, so a
second snoop is answered from the stale bytes.

## Reach on jet

jet's pair fabric is serial: one coherence transaction at a time, the memory
read after the snoop. A hot-set soak phase (both CPUs, 70 % writes, `WAYS + 3`
tags in two sets) run once on jet's pair rig on 2026-11-24 passed 26 of 26
cells, with 7-29 WB snarfs per cache per full cell, against jet-prime's 21-102
and two failing cells. The window is narrower on jet; it is not shown closed.
The phase was not kept in jet's testbench (jet is frozen).

## Why jet's suites miss it

The same five reasons as jet-prime BUG-002 (jet-prime inherited them): the
rule is the MAS's, `jet_wb_buffer_tb.py` asserts it, the core rig has no
second writer, the pair rig's model restates it, and the uniform soak reaches
a WB snarf a handful of times per cell.

## The fix (2026-11-25, ported from jet-prime BUG-002)

The same three parts as jet-prime's [delta 05](../../../../../../../../projects/components/cache-ip/jet-prime-mesi-l1/docs/delta/05_wb_ownership.md),
written up for jet in its MAS ch02/03:

1. `jet_wb_buffer`: a snoop effect on an entry whose drain has not started
   ends it (out of the CAM, retired at the head undrained, `r_dead`); the
   head whose drain has started drains and is marked passed (`sn_passed_o`).
2. `jet_control`: `drain_start` is held while a WB-sourced snoop on the
   head entry (`sn_head_o`) is granted or in service (jet-prime's narrowed
   form, not its first one); `w_src_wb` excludes a passed entry.
3. `jet_pair_fabric`: two write-backs to one line are forwarded in
   presentation order (`aw_pres_q` / `aw_yield_q`). jet's write port is a
   different FSM from jet-prime's, so this part was rewritten, not patched.

The ports went in by patch from jet-prime's two commits (9a1617aa5,
e6957a314) with the prefix renamed; the DV and formal harness changes the
same way. Three hand fixes: the ported pair-rig debug lines that did not
fit jet's model, the rig's rejected init hunk, and
`drain_not_held_off_head`'s setup -- in jet's later passes a fill commit
can hold `sn_safe_o` low in A's BRESP cycle, so the grant slipped past the
retire; the scenario now waits for both commits inside a 120-cycle drain.

The measured baseline cannot move: the HPDcache comparison's jet rows ran
jet's pair rig with one injector and the snoop side idle
(`reference_l1_comparison.md`). With no snoop, no entry ends and the drain
hold never asserts; with one cache writing back, the order hold never
sees two AWs. Two-injector traffic on jet's board rig can change cycles --
that is the fix -- and has not been rerun.

## Verification (2026-11-25)

- jet full 149/149 from clean, 0 reruns. Every full pair-rig cell ends 1-15
  entries and drains 1-14 passed entries per cache.
- Control unit: the drain-progress watcher and `drain_not_held_off_head`;
  gate / func / full PASS.
- Mutations: M3 (jet-prime's first hold, every drain start held) fails all
  three control passes (the watcher and the scenario); M2 (entries never
  ended) fails 26 of 26 pair-rig full cells.
- **M1, the fabric's order hold removed: not caught by random traffic.**
  48 seeds over two cells (`s016w2 m02v1` and `m02v4`, lru), 0 mismatches:
  in every random jet run the two caches never presented same-line
  write-backs at once (`wb_same_line` 0). On jet's serial fabric the
  requester's line buffer stays busy until the absorption of the passed
  line lands, so the new owner cannot start its next miss, and the moment
  the absorption lands the passed head's AW is free and goes at the next
  idle -- long before the new owner can evict and drain the line.
- **Scenario (j), `WbSameLineOrder` (2026-11-26), reaches it.** A TB valve
  on the shared memory's AW channel (`tb_aw_hold`, the rig top only: a
  slow memory holding AWREADY low) keeps the write port on a third burst:
  cache1's W write-back while cache0 evicts X and cache1's write miss
  snoops it (passed, abs1 X), then cache0's read of Z dirty in cache1
  (abs0 Z), which wins the idle after abs1 X over cache0's waiting AW and
  holds the port while cache1 evicts X. Both AWs to X then wait at once;
  the arbiter gives the last winner (abs0) top priority, which puts cache1
  ahead of cache0. With the hold the scenario passes; with M1, both gate
  cells (`s016w2`, `s128w4`) fail four ways: the port-level order check,
  (j)'s order and memory checks, and the memory audit (memory ends with
  cache0's stale bytes over cache1's line). jet still has no fabric
  proof; jet-prime's `ap_wb_order` covers the rule on its fabric.
- Formal (flat sha1 dd3fa5995bfd): covers 25/25 and 7/7, `cp_wb_ended` at
  step 46, `cp_wb_passed_snoop` at step 48; the liveness BMC (depth 176)
  PASS in 3:10:16 and the safety BMC (depth 64) PASS in 4:22:49, both
  smtbmc boolector, 2026-11-26.
