# BUG-004: the grant's rotating pointer plus the per-line lock can starve a requester on a contended line

**Priority:** P2 -- unbounded wait (no data corruption); PRD criterion 3's
fairness obligation.
**Status:** open (filed 2026-11-27).
**Owner:** TBD
**Where:** `rtl/top/onyx_ccu.sv`, the grant arbiter (`r_rr`, last granted + 1,
over N_PORTS + 1 requesters under the per-line lock).

## What is wrong

A requester whose candidate line is locked becomes eligible only in the
cycle the lock drops, and then loses to any requester ahead of it in the
rotation that wants the same line. Other requesters' grants on other lines
can rotate the pointer so the competitor is always ahead: no state
accumulates, so the wait has no bound.

## Reproduction

Liveness harness `formal/onyx/onyx_ccu_live` (L1b, "a request is overtaken
on its own line at most N times"): FAIL at step 15 (N = 2), 20 (N = 3), 25
(N = 4). N = 2 trace: cache 0 waits with a CleanUnique to line B from step
3; the IO, holding `io_arvalid` on line B, is granted it at steps 3, 8 and
13; cache 1's grants on other lines (steps 5, 9, 12) rotate `r_rr` to the
IO's turn each time; cache 0 is unlocked at steps 8 and 13 and loses both.
Real system: a device polling a flag line while CPU 0 waits to write it
and CPU 1 has any miss traffic. The cover reaches six overtakes in one
wait.

## Fix direction

Age or per-line fairness at the lock: e.g. a waiting requester that lost
its line's unlock cycle takes priority at the next unlock of that line.
Re-run L1b with a derived bound.
