# BUG-003: a stale write-back drain held behind a newer owner's absorption lands after it

**Priority:** P1 -- memory ends holding a line older than the last owner's
data; a later fill returns it.
**Status:** closed 2026-11-27 (fixed and sim-verified 2026-11-26; on the
fixed flat, md5 34a9908c, `prove_book` PASS 2026-11-26 and `prove`, BMC
depth 24, PASS 2026-11-27 in 18 h 53 min).
**Owner:** TBD
**Where:** `rtl/top/onyx_ccu.sv`, the write-back hold (`w_wb_blocked`) and
the absorption candidate (`w_absc_*`).

## What was wrong

A cache write-back to a line was held while a slot on that line gathered
or had an absorption pending. jet-prime BUG-002's fix made a victim whose
drain had started still drain after a snoop passed it on, relying on that
hold to put the drain after the absorption of the same bytes. The hold did
not ask WHOSE bytes the absorption carried: with a third requester (the IO
port here; a third cache would do the same), the stale drain could wait
out a later slot whose absorption was a NEWER owner's line, then land on
top of it.

onyx pair rig (two jet-primes + the IO port), full cell
`s016w2l64b64_m02v1_lru_mon_full`, seed 53050, line 0x1e3:

| cyc | event |
|---|---|
| 65545 | c1's victim drain starts (its AW presented) |
| 65552 | c0's ReadUnique snoops c1: the victim passes the line dirty (DT + PD) |
| 65573 / 65616 | c0's fill completes Modified; c0 writes beat 6 |
| 65617-18 | the IO's ReadOnce snoops both: c0 passes its line dirty, onyx absorbs it |
| 65680 | c1's drain -- held through both slots -- lands on top of c0's bytes |
| 65684 | c1's ReadUnique fills from memory: c1's old bytes, not c0's |

A second cell (`s016w2l64b64_m02v4_lru_mon_full`, seed 89177, line 0x1c3)
shows the same sequence. Pre-existing: nothing in the hold changed with
BUG-001 / BUG-002 (their timing exposed it in this run's seeds).

## Expected

The bytes land in data-age order: a past owner's drain before any newer
owner's absorbed line, which in turn lands before the newer owner's own
write-back.

## The fix

A write-back presented while a slot on its line still gathers is OLD (it
was presented before the line's current owner answered); `r_aw_old` marks
it for as long as it stays presented. An old write-back is not held behind
the line's pending absorption, and the absorption candidate waits while an
old write-back to its line is presented (`w_abs_old_wb`). A write-back
first presented after the verdict -- the new owner's -- is newer and still
waits for the absorption. No cycle: an absorption needs its slot's gather
complete, so a gather never holds an old write-back on that line while the
absorption waits for it.
