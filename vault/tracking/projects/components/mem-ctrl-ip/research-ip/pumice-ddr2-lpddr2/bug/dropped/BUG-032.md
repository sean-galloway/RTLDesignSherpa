**Priority:** P2
**Status:** dropped 2026-10-25 13:20 -- misfiled. The HEAD + harness-only build (bisect A) closes 75 MHz at +0.123 ns, 0 failing of 110 862, 45 473 LUT. The miss is the TASK-060 rewrite of `mc_wr_data_cam`; it is tracked there, not as a controller bug.
**Owner:** TBD
**Filed:** 2026-10-25 (found while building the TASK-060 write-combiner bitstream; attributed by an A/B build of the same text with `WR_MERGE_EN = 0`)
**Repro:** `cd projects/fpga-systems/NexysA7/mem-ctrl-ip-ddr2/build-perf && make clean && REGEN_BRIDGES=1 make prebuild && make bitstream`; read `fpga/reports/timing_failing_endpoints.csv` and `timing_failing_hotspots.txt`.

## Symptom

The pumice board bitstream (`ddr2_char_top`, Nexys A7-100T, 75 MHz) no
longer closes timing. Same text with the TASK-060 combiner compiled OUT
(`pumice_core.WR_MERGE_EN = 0`, nothing else changed):

| build | WNS | failing endpoints | top hotspots |
|---|---|---|---|
| combiner off (the pre-existing design) | -5.644 ns | 499 | u_meter_wr 128, u_chargen_regs 87, **u_arbiter 68**, u_page_policy 53, u_rd_cam 34 |
| combiner on | -5.365 ns | 2 571 | u_wr_cam 965, u_hist_rd 565, u_rd_cam 242, u_hist_wr 145, u_page_policy 108 |

The worst paths in both builds are the arbiter's `r_rd_pop -> r_wr_col_q` /
`r_wr_pre_q` / `r_wr_act_q` cone at 22-23 logic levels -- the same cone the
AXI_ID_WIDTH 8 -> 9 regression hit on 2026-09-13 (fixed 7baf98780 at +0.005
ns, 0 failing). Utilization 46 074 LUT (72.7 %) / 34 955 FF without the
combiner. The board runs: `make smoke` (leveled init, write_read, 128 MB
memtest) and 7 of 8 half-burst cells pass on the combiner bitstream, so the
miss is not (yet) functional, but a thin POSITIVE WNS is pumice's design
point and every quoted board number since the last closing build carries
this asterisk.

## Attribution is OPEN (correction, 2026-10-25 13:05)

The A/B above set `WR_MERGE_EN = 0` on TODAY'S text. That disables the merge
decision only; the rest of the TASK-060 rewrite of `mc_wr_data_cam` (strobes
moved from the BRAM into flops, the fill queue widened to carry the key, the
per-entry merge state, the done queue) and the injector / CSR changes stay in
the netlist. So the "combiner off" row is not the design that last closed at
75 MHz -- the owner reports the design was solid at 75 and converging on 100.
The clean baseline is the committed HEAD text, built from a detached worktree
(`RTLDesignSherpa-head-baseline`, ed3fed31c); its number decides whether the
regression is today's working tree or an earlier commit. Then, if HEAD closes,
today's changes are bisected in three steps: injector + CSR alone, plus the
CAM rewrite with merge off, plus merge on.

## What is NOT the cause

- The TASK-060 combiner: the miss is there with it compiled out. The
  combiner ADDS pressure of its own (2 072 more failing endpoints, 965 of
  them in `u_wr_cam`, median slack -0.6 ns, +598 LUT) -- tracked under
  TASK-060 as the insert-time merge match to pipeline, not here.
