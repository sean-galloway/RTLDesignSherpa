## Resolution (2026-10-20) — CLOSED

Root cause: **CLOSE_ON_DRAIN auto-precharge in `mc_cmd_arbiter`**
(`bf984205e` 2026-10-14 + three follow-ups). The write-column AP term
(`wr_col_ap`, HEAD ~line 967) treats the youngest in-window column of a
(bank,row) as the last demand (`r_wr_pop==1 && r_wr_xpop==0`) and
auto-precharges it. In streaming page-hit traffic that fires every
NUM_ENTRIES bursts, so every run of 8 bursts (32 beats at BL_WORDS=4)
re-ACTs — exactly the observed `max_run=32` and the write_ceiling
`bp=102`. Proven by disabling the parameter: all five probe cells pass;
the entry-store rework commits were exonerated (pumice never elaborates
the entry store).

Fix: `CLOSE_ON_DRAIN` is now a `pumice_core` parameter, **default 0**
(open-page research SKU); the sched-plane hook passes it through.
`test_pumice_close_drain` skips when the feature is off (env
`PUMICE_CLOSE_ON_DRAIN=1` rebuilds with it on and the probe still runs —
verified passing). Full regression after the fix: the five cells pass
and `test_pumice_top_row_hit_counters` (BUG-025) goes green as
collateral.

Left open for the product cores / granite spec: whether the AP term
should be gated on `page_policy_i != PAGE_POLICY_OPEN` so the feature
and explicit open-page mode compose.
