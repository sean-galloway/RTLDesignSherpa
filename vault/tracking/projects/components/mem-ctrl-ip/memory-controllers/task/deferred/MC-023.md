## 1. The self-close through the record path (MC-020's open term)

What is known (2026-11-22 proxy traces, 2026-11-23 reading): on a same-bank
close-page chain the head of the SAME row reads as a hit until the row image
clears the cycle after the self-close fires, then the record and the request
register follow -- fire -> ACT 4 slots. The head after a burst's last column
is of the NEXT ROW (col_major steps a row per burst), a miss from its first
record, and the lookahead lands its ACT at the timer: the "one in four exact"
chains. The committed model has neither: the `same_row` rule requests at the
timer (fire -> ACT 2) and the different-row head gets the lookahead, so the
close-page read sides are 10 .. 15 percent high (col_major_bl4 105 against
the board's 95, row_thrash 395 / 354, random 311 / 283, bank_interleave
col_major_interleaved_bl4 402 / 361). The `ap_close_rec` probe, restricted on
2026-11-23 to heads of the closed row, lands all four (92 / 352 / 280 / 358)
but takes the close_page mixed cell from 73 / 117 to 60 / 66 (the board 73 /
121): on that cell the same-row head is NOT present at the fire the way the
probe sees it, or its ACT is armed by something the probe gates. Measure on
the proxy (`PERF_STORE_TRACE` / `PERF_PICK_PROBE` at a close_page mixed
turn) which, then make the probe the term.

### Item 2 result (2026-11-23)

The gate was the self-close lookahead guard in the ACT request decode:
`m_ap_seq && m_clk - m_ap_fire_clk[b] < ap_close_lag * slot` with
`ap_close_lag` 0 was meant to be off, but `m_ap_fire_clk` is set at the
column's fire to the self-close's FUTURE fire slot, so while the close is
pending the difference is negative and the compare true -- the request
skipped, the lookahead lost, ACT at fire + 4 for a bank ready at fire + 3.
Gated on `ap_close_lag > 0` (`granite_sku_controller.cpp`). The committed
model then reproduces all eight 2026-11-22 `_rpap_` / `_rps_` CSVs to the
cycle (120 of 120 cells): bank_interleave col_major_bl4 446 / 520 (board
442 / 516), close_page mixed 73 / 121 (board 73 / 121). The CSVs did not
predate the term; the term had the defect, and the MC-020 record's section 9
is corrected in its section 10. Model tests 45 / 45. The ISSUE-005 / 007 GO
sweeps were re-run on the fixed model (their CSVs replaced; the ISSUE-005
record's section 9 is the re-read). Consequences for the other items:

- item 1: the `ap_close_rec` figures above (92 / 352 / 280 / 358) were the
  defective model's. On the fixed model the probe over-prices every
  close_page read side by 34 .. 50 percent (40 in / 17 out) and leaves the
  mixed cells unmoved. The proxy measurement stays the next step.
- item 4: the fixed model is in the read-first fast mode on 40 / 40 GO
  phases of BOTH presets (`open_page` 308 / 517 .. 525, `defaults` 309 / 560
  .. 571); the board is fast on 2 / 10 and 0 / 10. The defect had delayed the
  reads' re-ACT after the drain self-close and so imitated part of the
  board's race; the slot the model lacks is now on both presets.
- item 3: unchanged, 584.6 on all 120 directions.

The fixed model against the SKU board set: 54 in / 3 out (defaults and
open_page mixed_bl8 by mode, bank_interleave col_major_interleaved_bl4's
read +11.5).
