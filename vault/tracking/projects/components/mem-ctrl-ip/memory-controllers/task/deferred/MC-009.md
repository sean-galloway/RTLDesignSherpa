## Why

`mc_cmd_arbiter` recomputes eligibility, populations and order overlays from the CAM's flat vectors every cycle and feeds the STAGE-1b selection straight back into the STAGE-1a masks (02_scheduler_plane finding 10). The review's reading: (a) registering the 1b shadows breaks the loop and is what the clock needs on its own; (b) per-entry row hit / same-row-behind / sibling flags computed once at insert in the CAM instead of 16x16 every cycle, and (c) `col_sel/row_sel/QoS` as build-time choices, are the ~2k of 3,426 LUT. Throughput stays one command per host cycle (finding, section 2): any pipelining that keeps 1/cycle costs nothing the board can see.

## Plan

1. (a) Register the STAGE-1b selection and the guard vectors it feeds back; re-derive the forward guards for the extra cycle (the arbiter already carries `w_inflight_*` and `r_guard*` for one register of skew -- extend to two). Verify with the formal arbiter proof (ISSUE019 tRRD/tFAW, BUG033 tRFC, the TASK-047 lane contract) and the pumice JEDEC-replay cells before any area work.
2. (c) Build-time pick modes: `sched_order_mode / row_sel / col_sel / qos_en` as parameters with the CSR path kept behind `HAS_RUNTIME_PICK_MODES` for the research configuration.
3. (b) Hit and sibling flags into the CAM as registered per-entry bits, updated on insert and on the fire events the CAM already sees.
4. Routed A/B sys75 after (a), then after (c), then (b); the worst-path report must stop starting in `g_arbiter.u_arbiter`.

formal `mc_cmd_arbiter` prove / cover / dual all green; pumice func-both; the JEDEC replay cells; the char harness `windows` at 50 MHz on the board; and the sys75 routed build: WNS positive or the next cone named.
