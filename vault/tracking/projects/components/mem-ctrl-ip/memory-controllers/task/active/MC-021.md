## What was measured (the proxy at 100 MHz, lane on, pool 16)

1. **The bank timer, then two cycles.** `PERF_PICK_PROBE` under `close_page`:
   `bank_act_ready_i[b]` rises at cycle 13, the decode's `r_req` for the
   pair at 14, the winner register and the fire at 15; the column after it
   fires at tRCD exactly (the hint pre-arms it). Every period.
2. **The rank timer, then one more.** Under the interleaved close-page
   preset, ACT -> ACT on the main slot is 3 cycles in 80 percent of the pass
   against `t_rrd` = 2: `mc_global_timers` registers `trrd_ok`, so the
   counter installed at the fire reads <= 1 one cycle later and the ok is
   seen the cycle after that. JEDEC tRRD for the part is 10 ns, one MC cycle.
3. **The winner that waits blocks the ready column.** The main winner
   register holds an ACT while the lookahead says its timer opens within the
   pipe; the previous ACT's column, ready at +tRCD, fires two cycles late
   (40 percent of ACT -> COL gaps are 4). It reorders the pair; it does not
   lengthen the period.
4. **Two empty slots after each column pair.** The select after a column
   tries the column class first (CDIR, stale pairs only), takes the row
   class the next cycle, loads the winner, fires the cycle after: two slots
   with an ACT request pending and nothing firing.
5. **The lane carries none of it.** `w2_fire = w2_ok && w2_timer_ok &&
   w_col_fire_now` needs a column fire and an open tRRD in the same cycle;
   with the columns firing at +1 / +2 after an ACT, inside the tRRD window,
   the lane held one pair for a 64-cycle window and never fired. The main
   slot took every ACT.

## What

1. **The model term first** (`granite_sku_controller.cpp`): `hop_lag`
   (default 2 slots) -- a command whose timer the hint did not pre-arm fires
   no earlier than `hop_lag` slots after that timer opens; the hinted hop
   (a column after its own ACT) is exempt; and the rank-timer registered ok
   as one extra slot on ACT -> ACT. Validate on the three board presets:
   `close_page` 15 cells and `bank_interleave` 12 cells are the gate (today
   1 / 15 and 0 / 12 in tolerance); the 30 SKU cells must not move more than
   the 0.7 percent they sit at. The knob grid's method
   (`model_knob_grid_2026-11-17.md`) is the acceptance check: one setting,
   not one per preset.
2. **Then the RTL question**, priced in the model before any RTL is touched:
   can `bank_act_ready_la` / the hint reach the post-precharge ACT and the
   tRRD window (items 1, 2), and can the select skip the stale column class
   after a column pair (item 4)? Each is a `hop_lag` of 0 in the model for
   that hop; the model says what the close-page presets gain and whether the
   open-page SKU moves at all. Any RTL change runs `TEST_DUAL` both ways and
   the formal tasks under `formal/mc_common/mc_bank_pick`, and must stay in
   the 100 MHz cone the MC-014 lane was cut to fit.

## Step 1 landed (2026-11-17): the pipeline, not a lag

`model_mc021_pick_pipeline_2026-11-17.md`. The `hop_lag` form (plus a
registered rank ok, two empty slots after a column, a sticky winner) gave a
rigid eight-slot period per two beats on the eight-bank chain (195 MB/s
against the board's 234); the RTL probe (`pick_p9`, 64 cycles) shows six-,
seven- and eight-slot periods decided slot by slot by the self-stale mask. So
`granite_sku_controller.cpp` now carries `mc_bank_pick`'s stages: the decode's
request register (one slot behind the heads, lookahead `pick_la` = 2), the
winner register (held / speculated / fresh pick, the RTL's two select
branches, the rotor past the effective winner), the self-stale mask (row
command or column with nothing same-row behind; the fired column's pair when
nothing is filled behind it; the lane's pair under its three rules), the
next-op hint (t <= LA, t > LA - 1), the lane as a second winner register fired
beside a main-slot column, the registered rank ok (`rank_ok_lag` 1, the
model's own view of the last four ACT slots so the bank timers stay live), the
direction register in register form. `admit_lag` moved 4 -> 3: the fourth
cycle ISSUE-004 measured to the request IS the request register.

Two rules replaced two of the write-up's items: (1) the ACT after an
auto-precharge is armed by the lookahead when the next head MISSES the closed
row (the decode's miss branch, `mc_bank_timer` LA_AP on the bank-pick core:
the SKU's drain close, which the `col_major` cells need -- 283 / 390 against
283 / 389 on bl4) and requested at the timer when it HITS it (close page: the
measured hop); (2) the "two empty slots after a column pair" are the stale
re-pick of the fired pairs, zero to two per pair as the mask engages.

Result (board-compare `compare_model_sku_vs_board_sku100def_mc021_2026-11-17.md`,
45 in / 12 out): SKU 30 / 30 (mean 0.8 percent, mixed +1.7); `close_page`
14 / 15 (every single-bank cell within 1.6 percent; out: col_major_interleaved
_bl16, the board's reads a third faster than its writes, not modelled);
`bank_interleave` 1 / 12 at -13 .. -17 on the row-hit cells.

engages depends on how many schedulable entries a bank holds at its fire. The

10 215, pool 12 208, pool 16 199-202 against the board's 234. Not tuned. Two things to settle:
the exact masking of the held lane pair (the probe shows it masked in cycles

The two RTL questions, priced in the model (section 6): `ap_act_la` (arm the

## Step 2 landed (2026-11-18): the ACT spacing was a count, not a pipeline (memory-controllers BUG-004)

The owner asked why `bank_interleave` "sucks this badly" (238 MB/s of an 800
peak on the board). It is not the scheduler: the DRAM trace of the proxy's
bank_interleave run spaces ACTs 3 MC cycles apart (min 3, 52k of 65k gaps),
30 ns against the part's tRRD of 10 ns. The host programs the JEDEC distance
with its DFI phase margin (2), and `mc_global_timers` enforced a programmed N as
N+1 -- the convention pumice TASK-034 measured, documented and kept, calling the
prize small. In close page every 8-byte column needs its own ACT, so the ACT
rate is the throughput (267 MB/s ceiling at 3 cycles), and the DUAL_ISSUE lane,
which fires only beside a column that lands at ACT + tRCD = 2, could never be
timer-ok on a column slot: zero lane fires in 65,810 ACTs. Step 1's
`rank_ok_lag 1` was this N+1 carried into the model.

Fixed as BUG-004 (the compares open one cycle earlier; formal re-pinned at
exactly N; host `rtw_guard` 7 keeps the PHY-measured tRTW spacing; model
`rank_ok_lag 0`, preset nRTW 42). Model price before the RTL was touched: 199
-> 388; proxy after: bank_interleave 235 / 230 -> 387 / 385 MB/s, ACT gap 2,
lane fires 43,624 of 65,844. The model with the fixed default against the
PRE-fix board set (`compare_model_bug004_vs_board_prefix_sku100def_2026-11-18.md`,
40 in / 17 out) is the prediction the board re-run is read against: the SKU's
30 cells unchanged (mixed 284 / 284 union 569, +4.7), close_page single-bank
unchanged, bank_interleave +40 .. +67 percent, close_page interleaved +35 ..
+60, close_page row_thrash +21 / +40, random +15. Board (`board_bug004_granite_sku100_2026-11-18.md`):
bank_interleave 238 -> 387 on every row-hit cell, close_page col_major_interleaved_bl4
235 / 232 -> 379 / 387, everything else within 0.2 percent, 0 mismatched; the
model against the fixed set is 45 in / 3 out, bank_interleave row-hit +0.3 .. +0.5.
The residual is now the read / write asymmetric col_major cells (bank_interleave
col_major_bl4 board 258 / 351 vs model 333 / 333, col_major_bl8 387 vs 333,
col_major_interleaved_bl16 387 vs 347; close_page col_major_interleaved_bl8
251 / 328 vs 287 / 320): the read path after a read AP, named in step 1,
still unmeasured.

ISSUE-002 / ISSUE-003 re-read: the bank_interleave "3x" of ISSUE-002 was two
things -- the host preset is close page (the config error, closed into this
task) AND this count; ISSUE-003's "un-hinted command fires two MC cycles after
its timer opens" was the pipeline (step 1) plus, on every ACT, this cycle.

## Step 3 landed (2026-11-19): the lane timed at its slot, and the bl4 residual named

Item 1 below was probed on the proxy before anything was tuned
(`char_results/model_mc021_lane_slot_2026-11-19.md`). The proxy is the board
on every `bank_interleave col_major` cell (bl4 258.0 / 353.2 against 258 / 351,
bl8 386.5 / 386.1, bl16 386.8 / 385.1), and the asymmetry is the DRAM's
write-recovery hop over four banks: under `bank_lsb` 2 the family's 16 KiB
stride puts a burst's AxLEN beats on banks 0 .. AxLEN-1, so bl4 rotates four
banks and the write AP -> ACT hop (t_wr 4 + t_rp 2 = 6) spaces its ACTs 2, 2, 2,
6 (267 MB/s ceiling), while bl8 / bl16 use all eight and sit at the tRRD
ceiling. Not the read path, not the write fill.

The model's error was its own: it timed the DUAL_ISSUE lane's ACT in CK from
the odd tick it fires on, so a lane -> main ACT hop cost 5 CK where the RTL's
MC-cycle counters allow 2 cycles (3 CK on the wire; the host's phase margin).
Under the close-page interleave two of three ACTs ride, so the model ran 2.4
slots per beat against the RTL's 2.0 (bl8 333 against 387). Fixed in
`granite_sku_controller.cpp`: the lane's timing is checked at the slot tick and
the DRAM stamped with it -- every hop from a lane command is then the RTL's
count of MC cycles, checked hop by hop. Compare against the board set
`sku100bug004b_2026-11-18`: 53 / 4 -> 55 / 2; bl8 / bl16 389 against 387; bl4
283 / 389 against 258 / 351; the SKU's 30 cells moved 0.6 percent at most
(mixed union 569 -> 580, board 537). Tests 36 / 36, vendor suites 217 / 217.

The two still out are the bl4 read sides (+10.9, +11.3 against 10). The pick
probe beside the model's slot debug shows the same rules settling into
different fixed points on the four-bank rotation: the RTL's un-rideable ACT
takes the main winner register and holds through the tRRD wait, twice per four
beats, and the hinted columns lose their one-cycle hint under the hold (a
quarter of the read columns fire at ACT + 5 / + 7); the model holds once per
three lane ACTs and lands every column at tRCD. The separating term is the ACT
request after a READ auto-precharge was the first suspect and is cleared: both
probes (27 read, 20 write auto-precharges) give the RTL's bank hops as the model
has them (read 5 or 3, write 6, the request visible one cycle before the ready
rises = the model's `pick_la` 2 register). A trial knob that put the write
cells on the board was a knob, not a term, and was removed. The residual is the
attractor: the RTL's steady 11-13-cycle read pattern with one 3-gap from the
phase's first cycles against the model's 8-cycle one.

## Step 4 landed (2026-11-19): the record's hit lag, and the bl4 attractor explained

Item 1 below was the replay (`char_results/model_mc021_hit_lag_2026-11-19.md`):
the proxy's pick probe anchored on the second direction flip (the absolute
cycle moves 900 cycles between runs; the flip count does not) beside the
model's slot debug from its first slot. The separating term is a register, not
a rule: the head record's row-hit flag is classified against the bank timers'
REGISTERED row image and registered into the record, and the pick registers
the decode once more, so a bank's column request is visible three cycles
after its ACT (72 of 72); the model read the hit live (ACT + 2). The hinted
column fires at ACT + 2 in both, so the term binds only where a hold discards
the hint -- and that is the attractor: the leftover column request of a pair
whose column just fired wins the select (the rotor starts past the fired
pair), wastes the slot, masks the pair's fresh ACT from the lane for a cycle,
the select falls through to a row pick that holds the main register under
tRRD and discards the lane's hint, and the hintless column lands at ACT + 5
instead of the live-read model's + 3, pushing its bank's next ACT past the
8-cycle rotation slot (tRC 7). `hit_lag` 2 in `granite_sku_controller.cpp`;
the model then matches the RTL's hop distributions row for row (ACT -> column
2 / 5 / 7 at 74 / 18 / 7 percent, same-bank ACT -> ACT 7 / 8 / 9 / 11, 2.17
cycles per beat). Compare 55 / 2 -> 56 / 1: `col_major_bl4` 258 / 355 against
258 / 351, `col_major_interleaved_bl4` 247 / 246 against 242 / 253; the SKU's
30 cells moved 0.7 percent at most (`mixed_bl8` 2.1, toward the board). Tests
37 / 37, vendor 217 / 217.

The one cell out is new: `close_page col_major_interleaved_bl16` read side
-15.2 against 15 (model 191 / 204, board 171 / 240; bl8 284 / 302 against
251 / 328 is in at +13.4 / -7.8). The proxy on both cells is the board (bl16
171 / 232, bl8 248 / 320 from the DRAM trace). It is another regime: an
AxLEN-16 burst against the 16-entry pool, one or two heads in flight, the
same-row head after the auto-precharge; the RTL's reads there keep their hint
(ACT -> column 2 for 87 percent) where its writes pay the record path on every
second column, and the model's reads pay it (68 of 148 at + 5). Item 1 below.

The RTL question in its measured shape (item 3 below) is priced: masking the
fired auto-precharging column's pair for the cycle after its fire
(`ap_fire_stale` in the model) recovers the whole 10 percent on the four-bank
cells (to the tRRD floor 283 / 389), a quarter to a third of the interleaved
read sides, nothing the SKU would miss (`mixed_bl8` -2.4, toward the board).
