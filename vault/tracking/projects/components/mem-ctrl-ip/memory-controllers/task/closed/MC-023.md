## 1. The self-close through the record path (MC-020's open term)

What is known (2026-11-22 proxy traces, 2026-11-23 reading): on a same-bank
close-page chain the head of the SAME row reads as a hit until the row image
clears the cycle after the self-close fires, then the record and the request
register follow -- fire -> ACT 4 slots. The head after a burst's last column
is of the NEXT ROW (col_major steps a row per burst), a miss from its first
record, and the lookahead lands its ACT at the timer: the "one in four exact"
chains. The committed model has neither: the `same_row` rule requests at the
timer (fire -> ACT 2) and the different-row head gets the lookahead, so the
close-page read sides are 10 .. 15 percent high (col_major_bl4 105 against
the board's 95, row_thrash 395 / 354, random 311 / 283, bank_interleave
col_major_interleaved_bl4 402 / 361). The `ap_close_rec` probe, restricted on
2026-11-23 to heads of the closed row, lands all four (92 / 352 / 280 / 358)
but takes the close_page mixed cell from 73 / 117 to 60 / 66 (the board 73 /
121): on that cell the same-row head is NOT present at the fire the way the
probe sees it, or its ACT is armed by something the probe gates. Measure on
the proxy (`PERF_STORE_TRACE` / `PERF_PICK_PROBE` at a close_page mixed
turn) which, then make the probe the term.

### Item 1 result (2026-11-25, `char_results/model_mc023_ap_row_lag_refresh_drain_2026-11-25.md`)

The proxy's close_page incremental read chain (one generator): ACT, RD + AP at
+ 2, the self-close at ACT + tRAS 5, the next ACT at + 9 MC -- fire -> ACT 4;
the model had 8 (its same-row rule requested at the timer). Term `ap_row_lag`
2, the SKU default (`sku_noaprowlag` the model before): a head of the closed
row decodes no ACT before fire + 2; a head of another row keeps the
lookahead. Every close_page read side lands (incremental 104 -> 93, board 93;
col_major_bl4 105 -> 95, board 95; row_thrash 390 -> 358, board 354; random
310 -> 282, board 283), the mixed cell does not move, bank_interleave
col_major_interleaved_bl4's read 394 -> 361 (board 361). 57 in / 0 out, the
largest delta 12.5 -> 4.4 percent (three bank_interleave row_major reads,
+2.3 -> +4.4: item 3's cells). The `ap_close_rec` probe had gone the other
way because it lifted the no-lookahead before the fire. Model tests 49 / 49.

## 2. The bank_interleave bl4 read chain under `ap_seq` (the addendum to MC-020)

### Item 2 result (2026-11-23)

The gate was the self-close lookahead guard in the ACT request decode:
`m_ap_seq && m_clk - m_ap_fire_clk[b] < ap_close_lag * slot` with
`ap_close_lag` 0 was meant to be off, but `m_ap_fire_clk` is set at the
column's fire to the self-close's FUTURE fire slot, so while the close is
pending the difference is negative and the compare true -- the request
skipped, the lookahead lost, ACT at fire + 4 for a bank ready at fire + 3.
Gated on `ap_close_lag > 0` (`granite_sku_controller.cpp`). The committed
model then reproduces all eight 2026-11-22 `_rpap_` / `_rps_` CSVs to the
cycle (120 of 120 cells): bank_interleave col_major_bl4 446 / 520 (board
442 / 516), close_page mixed 73 / 121 (board 73 / 121). The CSVs did not
predate the term; the term had the defect, and the MC-020 record's section 9
is corrected in its section 10. Model tests 45 / 45. The ISSUE-005 / 007 GO
sweeps were re-run on the fixed model (their CSVs replaced; the ISSUE-005
record's section 9 is the re-read). Consequences for the other items:

- item 1: the `ap_close_rec` figures above (92 / 352 / 280 / 358) were the
  defective model's. On the fixed model the probe over-prices every
  close_page read side by 34 .. 50 percent (40 in / 17 out) and leaves the
  mixed cells unmoved. The proxy measurement stays the next step.
- item 4: the fixed model is in the read-first fast mode on 40 / 40 GO
  phases of BOTH presets (`open_page` 308 / 517 .. 525, `defaults` 309 / 560
  .. 571); the board is fast on 2 / 10 and 0 / 10. The defect had delayed the
  reads' re-ACT after the drain self-close and so imitated part of the
  board's race; the slot the model lacks is now on both presets.
- item 3: unchanged, 584.6 on all 120 directions.

The fixed model against the SKU board set: 54 in / 3 out (defaults and
open_page mixed_bl8 by mode, bank_interleave col_major_interleaved_bl4's
read +11.5).

### Item 4 progress (2026-11-24, `char_results/proxy_mc023_refresh_lock_2026-11-24.md`)

(`open_page` fast at tREFI 781 of 778 .. 782, `defaults` at 778). The race is

### Item 4, the refresh drain (2026-11-25, same record)

The RTL's override drains one PREpb per two slots (the registered row image
re-picks the bank the last PRE closed; the live gate refuses it), the REF at
the last PRE + tRP: proxy traces, every episode. The model's one PREab is
cheaper. Term `ref_drain_pb` (`sku_refdrainpb`, default OFF): on the 40 GO
phases `open_page` goes from the intermediate 290 / 367 x40 to the board's
two modes, 280 / 280 x27 and 302 / 579 x13 (board 5 / 3 of 8 aligned
offsets); `defaults` goes from 278 / 278 x27 (+ fast x13) to 293 / 408 x21
and 301 / 569 x19, never the board's one mode. So the committed `defaults`
held its mode by compensation, and a hybrid-path cost is still missing; of
four one-knob probes only `pred_cmd_reg` false moves it toward the board
(granite builds it true): the policy's history across a refresh episode is
the next place to measure. Default stays off until `defaults` holds with it.
Also: per-offset model-vs-board mode matching is not a test (a 10-MC launch
shift flips the mode on both presets); compare proportions.

### Item 4, the hybrid's term (2026-11-25, `char_results/model_mc023_hybrid_behind_lag_2026-11-25.md`)

The proxy built the research combiner (`STORE_MERGE_EN` 1) by default, not the
SKU; fixed (default 0). On the SKU proxy `defaults` is slow 280 / 280 on 10 of
16 offsets, fast on 6. The mode is the direction register's: slow = every run
256 columns; a refresh in the cap cycle eats the leftover column (a 255 run,
real on the RTL too) and the turn phase never recovers. With the drain alone the
model walked into that window every ~42k cycles because one refresh cost per
landing phase differed: after a REF the last column of the interrupted burst
auto-precharged in the model and not on the RTL, whose `behind_empty` sees an
entry a cycle after `r_rq_cnt` does. Term `behind_lag` (default 0): with
`ref_drain_pb` and `behind_lag` 1, `defaults` is 280 / 280 on 20 of 40 GO phases
and the RTL's fast mode on 20, no third mode; `open_page` unchanged in its
two. Not the default yet: the drain puts four bank_interleave write sides at
~518 (53 in / 4 out), the board's ISSUE-007 level in the wrong proportion -- item 3.
Open beside it: the board is slow on 8 of 8 aligned offsets where the RTL proxy
is fast on 6 of 16 (board vs simulation, not the model).
