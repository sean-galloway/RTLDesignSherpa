# MC-017: granite's `mixed` window is a direction-policy question -- one direction holds the bus for ~50 000 cycles -- fixed: the waiting direction got five cycles and no columns

> Filed from MC-016 (2026-11-15): the queue shape is not where granite's `mixed`
> gap is. The model's strict per-bank FIFO at granite's own pool depth reaches
> 750 MB/s both directions together; granite's sim cell at that depth is 600,
> its board cell 599-668, and the read side's worst latency in the sim is
> 52 120 cycles at pool 8 and 52 860 at pool 16 -- the whole of the other
> direction's run.

**Priority:** P3 -- one family (`mixed`), and MC-013 item 2 already found the
model's best drain policy to be the deep end granite sits at. The number to
move is the union, both directions over the longer window.
**Status:** CLOSED 2026-11-15 (active 2026-11-15; flips counted, cap swept, mechanism probed and fixed in `mc_bank_pick`, built and measured on the board the same day)
**Owner:** claude (mem-ctrl session)

## Why

Granite's `mixed` cells (4 writers + 4 readers from one GO, rows 8 apart in
shared banks) are asymmetric and bimodal: the board gives 487 / 300 on the
`defaults` preset and 334 / 649 on `open_page` at pool 8, and the two presets
swap shapes at pool 16 (318 / 649, 473 / 286;
`char_results/board_mc015_write_pool_sku100_2026-11-13.md`). The sim shows
the same shape with `rd_lat_max` of 52 120 cycles (pool 8, lane on,
`scratchpad mc014/perf_g5_mixed.json`) and 52 860 (pool 16): whichever
direction holds the first batch keeps the bus until its own stream is nearly
done. The union -- 16.4 MB over the longer of the two windows -- is 600 (sim,
pool 8), 533 (sim, pool 16), 599 / 668 (board, pool 8), 635 / 571 (board,
pool 16). The model's strict per-bank FIFO with Ramulator's watermark drain
at the same depths reaches 750 / 698 (`sim_mc016_two_head_model_2026-11-15.md`),
and its best pooled policy 773-776 at every depth, so the 20-24 percent
between granite and its own shape in the model is the direction rule, not
the queue.

The rule (`mc_bank_pick`, the direction register): hold a direction while it
has pending columns, up to `GRANITE_DIR_BATCH_MAX` = 255 column fires; with
`GRANITE_SCHED_DIR_OWNS_ROWS` = 1 and `GRANITE_SCHED_DIR_HOLD_IDLE` = 8
(granite TASK-003, 2026-11-01) the owner may also close the other direction's
rows and holds through idle gaps of up to eight cycles. On `mixed` the owning direction never runs dry (four injectors keep
its heads queued), so the cap is the only flip, and after the flip the other
direction holds for its own 255. A 255-column batch is 2 KB of BL4 columns
per direction per turn -- long enough that the write pool (8 or 16 entries)
stalls the AXI writers for the whole read batch and vice versa, which is the
`rd_lat_max` and the asymmetry.

## What

1. Measure the flips. `PERF_PICK_PROBE` samples the direction state from the
   first cap; add a per-run count of `dbg_dir_flip_o` by `dbg_dir_cause_o`
   (0 old-direction fire, 1 drained, 2 cap) with the batch length histogram
   (`dbg_dir_batch_o`) on `mixed` at pool 8 and 16, lane on.
2. Sweep the cap on the proxy at 16 / 32 / 64 / 128 / 255 at both pool
   sizes and read the union, the two directions, and `rd_lat_max`. The cap
   is `GRANITE_DIR_BATCH_MAX` (`granite_pkg`, 255), tied to the plane's
   `sched_wr_batch_max_i` in `granite_top_geared` and read back in a CSR, not
   written through one; the sweep needs a proxy parameter the way
   `TEST_NUM_ENTRIES` reaches `NUM_ENTRIES` (MC-015). MC-013 item 2's model
   curve says deeper is better for a watermark drain; granite's batch rule is
   not a watermark, and the model's `open8` cells (750 strict, 773 pooled)
   are the target.
3. If a batch value lifts the union without touching the single-direction
   families (they never flip), it is a SKU default change for the owner
   ("THE FPGA SKU IS THE CONFIGURATION", 2026-11-08), not RTL. If none does,
   record the curve and close.

## Done when

The flip count and batch histogram sit beside the `mixed` cells in
`char_results`, the batch sweep is recorded at both pool sizes, and either a
default is proposed to the owner with its union number or the note says why
the direction rule is not the gap either.

## Results, sim half (2026-11-15)

`char_results/sim_mc017_direction_register_2026-11-15.md`.

1. **Flips counted** (`PERF_DIR_FLIPS`): at pool 8 and 16, lane on, the cap
   hands the register to the reads 143 times and 136 of those turns end
   within 5 cycles with zero columns -- a write column fires and takes it
   back with a fresh 255. The window is writes-then-reads; `rd_lat_max`
   52 120 cycles is the write side's run.
2. **Cap swept** (`TEST_DIR_BATCH_MAX` 255 / 128 / 64 / 32 / 16 at both pool
   sizes; `DIR_BATCH_MAX` threaded through `granite_top_geared`,
   `granite_char_macro`, the harness, the TB top and `GRANITE_DIR_BATCH_MAX`
   for the board): the zero-column turns persist at every cap (2 000+ at
   the small ones), unions 455-603 / 520-533. Not the lever; no default
   from this axis.
3. **The take-back, probed** (`PERF_PICK_PROBE_FLIP`): the owner's PRE closes
   the read's row in the flip cycle, the read's stale column pick dies, the
   writer's ACT on the same bank wins the row fall-through while the read's
   request is a decode cycle behind, and its hinted column fires ungated.
   Behind that: the drain flip read "no column pending" as drained, and
   with the lane on the read's first command landed one cycle after the
   idle hold expired.
4. **Fixed in `mc_bank_pick`** (four register-only terms; one tried and
   reverted -- the row fall-through gate cost the lane-off window 40 %):
   every turn carries its 255 columns; `mixed` 272 / 273 (union 545) at
   pool 8 lane on, 272 / 272 (543) at pool 16, 264 / 265 (528) lane off;
   `rd_lat_max` ~520 cycles everywhere (5 us, was 520 us). Union +1 % at the
   shipped SKU, +2 % at pool 16 + lane, -9 % at pool 8 + lane (the old
   sequential shape beats any fair schedule there; the model said so too).
   Single-direction cells within 2 MB/s; formal (equiv, equiv_n4, cover,
   dual, dual_cover) pass with the golden copy carrying the same change;
   granite top, pumice fub / macro / top / phy, common-ip benches pass.
