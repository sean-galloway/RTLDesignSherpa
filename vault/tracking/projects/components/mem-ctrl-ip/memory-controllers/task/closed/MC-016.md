# MC-016: a cross-bank row-hit-first pick for granite's `mixed` window (model the two-head store first) -- the second head is the same burst; the gap is the direction rule (MC-017)

> Filed from MC-013 item 4 (2026-11-12): the one scheduling policy the model prices
> at more than a few percent on granite's cells.

**Priority:** P3 -- one family (`mixed`), and the first step is a model, not RTL.
**Status:** CLOSED 2026-11-15 (active 2026-11-15; the model step answered it in one afternoon, no RTL proposed; the mechanism refiled as MC-017)
**Owner:** claude (mem-ctrl session)

## Why

On the Ramulator 2 DDR2 model at the sku100 point
(`ddr2-characterization/char_results/sim_mc013_policies_2026-11-12.md`, item 4), a
pooled queue that never preempts an open row's hits (upstream `FRFCFS-RowHit`)
moves `mixed_bl8` to 360 / 360 MB/s where strict per-bank FIFOs with a rotor and
a column-first select (`BankFIFO-RR`, the shape of granite's bank-FIFO store under
`mc_bank_pick`) give 320 / 320: -11 percent, from row conflicts at the FIFO heads
holding the hits queued behind them (177 888 conflicts against 161 758). Every
single-direction family ties within 1 MB/s, so the queue shape costs nothing
there. Granite measured 263 / 330 on `mixed` (sku100, 2026-11-09); on the
captured `mixed` stream (item 5) the 2 CK model reaches 370.

Granite's store is not the strict FIFO: `HEAD2_EN` keeps two head records per
bank, so the pick can see one record past a conflicting head. The RTL therefore
sits somewhere between the model's two columns and the exact gap is not known.

## Added 2026-11-13 (from MC-015): `mixed` against the pool size

MC-015 sized the store pool 8 -> 16 (the write ceiling). On `mixed` the sim
loses reads (475 / 300 -> 473 / 267 at the proxy's `BANK_CAP_MIXED` 6) and the
cap sweep at pool 16 is not monotonic: 120 / 120, 277 / 513, 473 / 267,
221 / 219 at caps 3 / 4 / 6 / 8. On the board the two presets swapped shapes
with the deeper pool (defaults 487 / 300 -> 318 / 649, open_page 334 / 649 ->
473 / 286; 0 mismatches), the window being bimodal on which direction holds
the first batch. The direction register holds a direction while it has
pending columns up to the 255 batch cap, and sixteen entries keep a side from
running dry. Whatever this task decides about the pick should be measured at
both pool sizes, and the cap / batch rule is part of the same question
(`char_results/sim_mc015_write_pool_2026-11-13.md`,
`board_mc015_write_pool_sku100_2026-11-13.md`).

## What

1. Model the two-head store: a `BankFIFO-RR` variant that offers each bank's two
   oldest requests (hit preferred) -- the overlay scheduler is 90 lines, the
   change is a second pass over the heads. Run `mixed` against `open32_rowhit`
   and the captured `mixed` trace.
2. If the two-head model still trails the pooled row-hit-first queue by more than
   a few percent on `mixed`: price a cross-bank row-hit preference in
   `mc_bank_pick` (the select already classes hits; a hit anywhere beating a miss
   at the rotor is one more term in the class order) and the FPGA cost of a
   deeper peek in `mc_bank_fifo_store`.
3. Otherwise record the number and close.

## Done when

The two-head model number sits beside the measured `mixed` cell in `char_results`,
and either an RTL change with its sim number is proposed as its own task or this
one records why the queue shape is not where granite's `mixed` gap is.

## Results (2026-11-15)

`char_results/sim_mc016_two_head_model_2026-11-15.md`; the model CSVs
`model_ramulator2_ddr2_<config>_2026-11-15.csv` and the captured-trace sweep
`model_ramulator2_ddr2_mixed_captured_mc016_2026-11-15.csv` beside it.

1. **The premise was wrong in the RTL.** `HEAD2_EN` is not a scheduling peek:
   `mc_bank_pick` reads the second record only under the one-cycle pop
   shadow (`w_cand = r_shadow ? rec2 : rec1`), i.e. in the cycle after it
   fired the head, to hide the record register's latency; outside the shadow
   it is never read, and its `hit` is the stored "same row as the head" bit,
   not a compare (the store's header says so). In scheduling terms granite's
   store IS the model's strict per-bank FIFO (`open*_bankrr_col`).
2. **A second-head peek would buy exactly nothing at AxLEN 8.** The overlay's
   `BankFIFO-RR` got a `peek_depth`: a bank whose head is a row conflict
   offers the oldest row hit among its first N entries. At N = 2, 4 and 8 the
   cells are bit-identical to the strict FIFO on the captured `mixed` trace
   and the rebuilt one, at pool 8, 16 and 32: the entries behind a head are
   its own burst's beats (one store entry per beat, eight to a burst, one
   row). A hit behind a conflict is at least eight deep; N = 16 at pool 32
   is the first depth that moves (342 -> 381 captured), and the whole queue
   374 / 357 / 375 at 32 / 16 / 8 (8-deep: still nothing, one burst fits).
3. **The queue shape is not where granite's `mixed` gap is.** Model union
   (both directions over the longer window, captured trace): strict FIFO
   750 / 698 / 684 at pool 8 / 16 / 32, pooled row-hit-first 773 / 776 /
   776. Granite at its own shape and depth: sim 600 (pool 8) / 533 (pool
   16), board 599-668 / 571-635, with `rd_lat_max` 52 120 / 52 860 cycles --
   one direction holds the bus for the other's whole run. The 20-24 percent
   between granite and the strict FIFO is three to six times the strict
   FIFO's distance to the best pooled queue (3-12 percent); it is the
   direction register's batch rule. Filed as **MC-017**.
4. The single-direction families do not move under any peek depth at any
   pool depth (the `random` reads +1 MB/s with the whole-queue peek). No RTL
   change proposed: a cross-bank row-hit preference already exists in the
   pick's class order (COL before ACT / PRE), and an intra-bank peek deep
   enough to matter is a burst-deep reorder in `gaxi_age_fifo_sync` (no
   mid-queue pop today) plus a row compare per record -- the cost the store's
   stored-bit form was chosen to avoid -- for a family the direction rule
   bounds first.
