# MC-023: the SKU model's three open fidelity terms -- the self-close's record path, the pick pipeline's refresh-relaunched orbits, the hybrid policy's mixed-cell mode

## 1. The self-close through the record path (MC-020's open term)

What is known (2026-11-22 proxy traces, 2026-11-23 reading): on a same-bank
close-page chain the head of the SAME row reads as a hit until the row image
clears the cycle after the self-close fires, then the record and the request
register follow -- fire -> ACT 4 slots. The head after a burst's last column
is of the NEXT ROW (col_major steps a row per burst), a miss from its first
record, and the lookahead lands its ACT at the timer: the "one in four exact"
chains. The committed model has neither: the `same_row` rule requests at the
timer (fire -> ACT 2) and the different-row head gets the lookahead, so the
close-page read sides are 10 .. 15 percent high (col_major_bl4 105 against
the board's 95, row_thrash 395 / 354, random 311 / 283, bank_interleave
col_major_interleaved_bl4 402 / 361). The `ap_close_rec` probe, restricted on
2026-11-23 to heads of the closed row, lands all four (92 / 352 / 280 / 358)
but takes the close_page mixed cell from 73 / 117 to 60 / 66 (the board 73 /
121): on that cell the same-row head is NOT present at the fire the way the
probe sees it, or its ACT is armed by something the probe gates. Measure on
the proxy (`PERF_STORE_TRACE` / `PERF_PICK_PROBE` at a close_page mixed
turn) which, then make the probe the term.

## 2. The bank_interleave bl4 read chain under `ap_seq` (the addendum to MC-020)

The committed model gives `granite_sku100_bi col_major_bl4` 446 / 446 with or
without the TIMINGS_RP_AP field; the board 442 -> 516, the model with
`ap_seq=false` 446 / 520, the 2026-11-22 CSV (written before the term was
finished) 446 / 520. The read ACT lands one slot past tRC (a seven-cycle
rotation of four banks for six). `ap_act_la`, `pick_la` 1 / 3, `hit_lag` 1
and `close_lag` 0 do not move it; the rows do step per burst in the model's
map. Read the decode at the ACT's slot (`GRANITE_SKU_DEBUG`, the read pass
at CK 200000 of that cell: the request appears at fire + 4 where the bank is
ready at fire + 3) and name the gate.

## 3. The pick pipeline's orbits after a refresh (ISSUE-007 step 3)

The proxy's bank_interleave row-hit stream runs 3 columns per 4 cycles and,
after some refreshes, 8 per 12 / 13 / 14 for whole refresh periods; the model
has the base orbit only (584.6 on 40 GO phases). Probe: `PERF_PICK_PROBE` on
the proxy at tREFI 779 across the refresh exits at cycles 63,260 (into the 8
per 12 orbit) and 62,481 (back to base), 160 cycles each; the register that
differs is the state the model lacks -- or, if the slow re-launch is a hold
the RTL need not take, a pick-core question for the owner.

## 4. The hybrid policy's mixed cell (ISSUE-005's fidelity note)

The board's `defaults mixed_bl8` is 277 / 276 on ten of ten runs; the model
at hold 8 lands there on 14 of 40 GO phases and in two modes the board never
shows on 26 (290 / 343, 305 / 489). Under `page_mode` 0 the model has the
board's pair and proportion, so the extra slot is in the hybrid's path (the
drain gate on the PRE request, `page_mode` 4). Measure the write head's miss
-> PRE -> fire chain on the proxy under `defaults` at a cap turn and carry
the slot. Also: the model's open_page fast mode reads 524 where the board
reads 594.
