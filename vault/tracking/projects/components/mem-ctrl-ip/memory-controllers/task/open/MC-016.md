# MC-016: a cross-bank row-hit-first pick for granite's `mixed` window (model the two-head store first)

> Filed from MC-013 item 4 (2026-11-12): the one scheduling policy the model prices
> at more than a few percent on granite's cells.

**Priority:** P3 -- one family (`mixed`), and the first step is a model, not RTL.
**Status:** open 2026-11-12
**Owner:** TBD

## Why

On the Ramulator 2 DDR2 model at the sku100 point
(`ddr2-characterization/char_results/sim_mc013_policies_2026-11-12.md`, item 4), a
pooled queue that never preempts an open row's hits (upstream `FRFCFS-RowHit`)
moves `mixed_bl8` to 360 / 360 MB/s where strict per-bank FIFOs with a rotor and
a column-first select (`BankFIFO-RR`, the shape of granite's bank-FIFO store under
`mc_bank_pick`) give 320 / 320: -11 percent, from row conflicts at the FIFO heads
holding the hits queued behind them (177 888 conflicts against 161 758). Every
single-direction family ties within 1 MB/s, so the queue shape costs nothing
there. Granite measured 263 / 330 on `mixed` (sku100, 2026-11-09); on the
captured `mixed` stream (item 5) the 2 CK model reaches 370.

Granite's store is not the strict FIFO: `HEAD2_EN` keeps two head records per
bank, so the pick can see one record past a conflicting head. The RTL therefore
sits somewhere between the model's two columns and the exact gap is not known.

## What

1. Model the two-head store: a `BankFIFO-RR` variant that offers each bank's two
   oldest requests (hit preferred) -- the overlay scheduler is 90 lines, the
   change is a second pass over the heads. Run `mixed` against `open32_rowhit`
   and the captured `mixed` trace.
2. If the two-head model still trails the pooled row-hit-first queue by more than
   a few percent on `mixed`: price a cross-bank row-hit preference in
   `mc_bank_pick` (the select already classes hits; a hit anywhere beating a miss
   at the rotor is one more term in the class order) and the FPGA cost of a
   deeper peek in `mc_bank_fifo_store`.
3. Otherwise record the number and close.

## Done when

The two-head model number sits beside the measured `mixed` cell in `char_results`,
and either an RTL change with its sim number is proposed as its own task or this
one records why the queue shape is not where granite's `mixed` gap is.
