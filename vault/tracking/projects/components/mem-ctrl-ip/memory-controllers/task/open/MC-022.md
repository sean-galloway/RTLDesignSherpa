# MC-022: the granite fire cone has no margin at 100 MHz -- a structural cut, priced in the model first

> Filed 2026-11-22 from memory-controllers MC-020 decision 2
> (`char_results/board_mc020_t_rp_ap_sku100_2026-11-22.md`, section 5): the
> RTL with the per-path auto-precharge tRP field (46 flops, one mux, none of
> it on a failing path) landed at WNS -0.052 and -0.054 ns under the 0.2 ns
> setup-uncertainty budget on two placements (the flow's default and
> ExtraTimingOpt), where the RTL before it had passed by +0.014 and +0.080;
> `ddr2_char_top.xdc` already records the sys100 band as +0.003 .. -0.159 ns
> across placer runs. The failing paths are the design's fire cone, 70-80
> percent route, 10-13 logic levels in ~2-3 ns of logic.

**Priority:** P2 -- the owner's rule (2026-11-08) is that WNS near zero is
placement noise, the 0.2 ns budget is what makes a build predictable, and a
design that lands at zero under it is fixed structurally, not by another
placement; every RTL change to the pick core or the store now flips the
sign of the SKU build, and MC-020's field waits on this to ship.
**Status:** OPEN 2026-11-22
**Owner:** TBD

Floorplanning is not the answer: the XDC records three Pblock attempts on this
cone (u_sched + u_ifc, the core, the arbiter alone) each making WNS worse --
"the fix is a pipeline register". The directive sweep is how the band is
re-measured, not how it is closed.

## What

A structural cut in the fire cone that the SKU model prices BEFORE the RTL,
as the lane's rule: each candidate is a model term (the model already carries
`pick_la`, `hit_lag`, `close_lag`, `wr_fill_lag` for the pipeline it has), its
cost on the `every` suite read against the board baseline, and the one with
the smallest cost built, proved against the pick's golden copy (the equiv /
equiv_n4 / dual tasks; an exact restructuring passes them untouched, a new
register stage replaces the gold with a dated note), TEST_DUAL both ways, the
sys100 build under the budget on two placements. Candidates, cheapest first:

1. **Exact restructurings, no behaviour change** (the gold copy stays):
   `rr_onehot`'s lowest-set-bit as two 8-bit halves and a mux instead of the
   16-bit increment (three CARRY4 off the path); the lane pick's class mux
   after two parallel rotors instead of before one; the duplicate aged
   filter (applied in the caller and again inside `rr_onehot`) removed.
   Worth perhaps a nanosecond on the pick-select class alone; the store
   class is untouched, so this cannot close the build by itself.
2. **The pop a cycle after the fire** (the store's queue write pointer from a
   registered fire): takes the fire decision off the LUTRAM write enable.
   A behaviour change: the next head record appears a cycle later, which
   the hinted column (ACT + 2) and `hit_lag` / `wr_fill_lag` would feel --
   the model prices it first.
3. **A request-register stage split** in the pick (the stale mask applied a
   cycle earlier, or the candidate set registered): `pick_la` + 1 in the
   model, the MC-021 measurements say what that costs on close-page chains.

## Done when

The decision-2 RTL (or its successor) closes at 100 MHz with WNS >= 0 under
the 0.2 ns budget on two placements, with the cut's cost on the `every` suite
inside the run-to-run band or priced and accepted in the record -- or the
task is closed as a recorded negative with the number that says why, and
the SKU ships at the frequency that number allows.
