## Why

Granite's 16 bank-queue FIFOs (`mc_bank_fifo_store`) are ~1,770 LUT, 32% of granite_core (row 2): one RAM64M per direction with 3-entry head shift registers holds the same contract. `mc_bank_pick`'s 16-way rotor over pair heads can be an 8-way per direction (row 15, 150-250 LUT, high risk: it is the pick order), A/B'd on the proxy families.

## Plan

1. Row 2: `mc_bank_fifo_store` bodies into one RAM64M per direction (reset-free, MC-005's rule), heads in 3-entry shift registers so the pick's decode still reads flops; the age-FIFO contract (TASK-002: pool age, thresh = oldest-in-pool) kept by the head records.
2. Row 15 only after row 2 is measured: 16->8 rotor, A/B on `mg_<family>` proxies and the board at 75 / 100 MHz.
3. Routed A/B granite sys75 and sys100 after each.
