# MC-019: the row lane carries ACT only -- a PRE never needs to ride

> Filed 2026-11-17 from memory-controllers MC-018's pattern x variant sweep
> (`char_results/model_mc018_sku_config_2026-11-17.md`, section 3): on the SKU
> model a lane that may carry PRE and ACT (`lane` 3, the SKU) and one that
> carries ACT only (`laneact`) give the same MB/s on all 29 patterns, and a
> lane that carries PRE only (`lanepre`) is the lane off to the MB/s on all 29
> (-6 .. -20 percent on the bank-parallel miss families). The auto-precharge
> closes the rows this traffic closes; a demand PRE in the main slot costs
> nothing the model can see.

> Re-read 2026-11-17 under the ISSUE-004 model (the store's admission terms added):
> `laneact` == the SKU and `lanepre` == `laneoff` on all 28 patterns of the
> re-run sweep (`model_sku_sweep_table_issue004_2026-11-17.md`); the claim stands.

## What

1. A `LANE_PRE` parameter (default 1 = today) or the plain removal, in
   `mc_bank_pick` and the golden copy under `formal/mc_common/mc_bank_pick`
   (`equiv`, `dual`, `dual_cover`), with the formal tasks passing.
2. Sim A/B on the granite proxy at the SKU, `TEST_DUAL` both ways (every
   pick-core change runs both), the `every` + `windows` families plus `mixed`:
   the cells must be unchanged within the run-to-run band, `mismatched` 0.
3. The no-knob `GRANITE_SYS_100=1 make bitstream`: LUT / FF and WNS beside
   the 2026-11-16 SKU build (37,357 LUT / +0.012 ns). If the lane's PRE path
   was on the binding cone the margin moves; if not, the task closes on the
   area number alone.

## Done when

Both builds of the pick core are bit-identical on the suites, the formal
tasks pass, the sys100 build closes under the 0.2 ns budget, and the write-up
carries the LUT and WNS delta. No bandwidth claim is made.

## Dropped (2026-11-20)

Owner's decision on the measurement: drop. The timing gain the task was filed
for did not materialise (WNS +0.014 against +0.012 / +0.080, the pick core
still the worst path), and 277 LUT does not buy 1.3 percent on a read side
the board measures. The RTL is at HEAD; the patched files stayed in the
session scratch only. What the task leaves behind is in ISSUE-006 (the
model's missing demand PREs) and granite ISSUE-003 (the proxy's band).
