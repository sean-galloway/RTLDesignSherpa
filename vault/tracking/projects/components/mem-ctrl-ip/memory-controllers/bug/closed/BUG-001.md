# BUG-001: mc_wr_intake drops write responses when the host stalls BREADY

> Source: found reading `mc_wr_intake` during memory-controllers ISSUE-001 (the
> FPGA microarchitecture review), 2026-11-05. Verified against the source, not
> yet reproduced in a test.

**Priority:** P1 -- a lost B is a protocol violation the master hangs on, and it
is silent at the controller.
**Status:** closed 2026-11-07 -- fixed 2026-11-06 (wr_done ready, bound applied at admission), regression-clean (see Closed).
**Owner:** TBD
**Where:** `projects/components/mem-ctrl-ip/common-ip/rtl/fub/mc_wr_intake.sv:445-459`.
Both controllers (`mc_axi4_plane` instantiates it for pumice and granite).

## Observed

The store's completion strobe `wr_done_valid_i` has no ready signal at the
intake port (`:149`) or at the plane (`mc_axi4_plane.sv:142`). It is ORed with
the ragged-burst error B into `w_b_wr_valid` and pushed into
`gaxi_fifo_sync u_b_fifo` with `DEPTH = B_FIFO_DEPTH = 4` (`:53`). The FIFO's
`wr_ready` (`w_b_wr_ready`) is declared, connected, and read by nothing.
`gaxi_fifo_sync` writes only on `wr_valid && wr_ready`
(`rtl/amba/gaxi/gaxi_fifo_sync.sv:18`), so a push into a full FIFO is dropped.

The B FIFO drains only on `fub_bready` (`:464`). The store drains and strobes
`wr_done` as the scheduler commits, with no view of B-side space. With 8
(pumice) or 16 (granite) write slots in flight, a host that holds `bready` low
across more than four completions loses every fifth and later B until it drains.

## Expected

Every accepted write produces exactly one B (AXI4 A3.4.1). Either `wr_done`
is back-pressured by B space, or the B path is sized to the number of writes
the intake can have accepted, or Bs are released from a queue that is bounded
by the same admission as the slots.

## Why no test caught it

The char engines and the BFM write masters keep `bready` high; the B FIFO never
fills. The fix needs a directed test with `bready` held low for more than
`B_FIFO_DEPTH` completions, then released, checking the B count and ids.

## Fix shape

The AW-order B release queue proposed for granite BUG-001 covers this too:
an id queue pushed at AW admit (bounded by the slot count), a per-slot
`drained` bit set by `wr_done`, Bs released from the queue head on
`bready`. `wr_done` then needs no ready (it only sets a bit), and the queue is
sized to the admission domain by construction. For pumice the same structure
sits on the CAM's `wr_done`. Minimal alternative: `B_FIFO_DEPTH >= NUM_ENTRIES`
plus a guard that fails elaboration when it is not.

## Fix (2026-11-06)

`wr_done` now has a ready. `mc_wr_intake` exports `wr_done_ready_o` (B FIFO
room and no error B taking the push this cycle), `mc_axi4_plane` forwards it,
and every core wires it back into its storage backend:

- **pumice / scoria / andesite** (`mc_storage_plane` -> `mc_wr_data_cam`): the
  done queue pops only on the intake's ready, and the bound is applied at
  ADMISSION: an outstanding-B counter (+1 per admitted host burst, -1 per B the
  intake takes) holds `ins_ready_o` at 4 x NUM_ENTRIES outstanding, and the done
  queue is sized to that bound so it never fills. The first cut back-pressured
  the DRAIN instead, and the pumice command-history checker caught a tRP
  violation at once: a drain that waits leaves a committed WRITE at the DFI
  head without its data and the PRE/ACT behind it collapse their spacing. The
  bound belongs at admission, never at the drain.
- **granite** (`mc_bank_fifo_store`): the AW-order B release queue (granite
  BUG-001) takes its entry at admit and pops on the intake's ready, so the
  bound is admission by construction.
- **entry store** (`mc_xddr2_entry_store`, sim reference backend): the drain's
  B-strobing beat waits for the ready. It is not the FPGA backend; the drain
  hold is accepted there.

Tests: `test_granite_core_b_stall` and `test_pumice_core_b_stall` hold BREADY
40 cycles per B under 24 streaming writes; every B must arrive and, as the
vacuity guard, the intake's 4-deep B FIFO must have been full at least once
(granite reads `u_axi_plane.u_wr_intake.w_b_wr_ready`). Both were RED on the
old RTL (granite: all 24 Bs lost after the fourth, master timed out).

## Closed (2026-11-07)

`test_granite_core_b_stall` and `test_pumice_core_b_stall` (BREADY held 40 cycles
per B under 24 streaming writes, intake B FIFO full at least once as the vacuity
guard) pass; both were RED on the old RTL. Regression on the full tree (2026-11-06, every suite from `make clean-all`, the
MC-004 / MC-005 array moves included): common-ip fub 47 / 47 and macro 36 / 36
+ 3 declared xfail (the store-arbiter contract cells, chopped bursts included);
granite top 30 / 30 + 1 declared xfail; pumice `run-all-func-both` 224 + 224
(default and board geometry); char harness 226 + 2 xfail; val/amba 1437;
val/cdc 131.
