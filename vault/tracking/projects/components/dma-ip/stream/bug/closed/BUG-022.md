# BUG-022: the scheduler sent a last descriptor's DESC_COMPLETE / IRQ MonBus packet once per cycle while its writes committed

**Status:** CLOSED 2026-10-24 -- fixed on filing in `rtl/fub/scheduler_core.sv` (packets on state ENTRY), the same defect as rapids BUG-016
**Priority:** P2
**Filed from:** stream TASK-020 (the stream-g equivalence bench found it: the FSM and the gatling-sequenced scheduler dwell in FETCH_DESC and COMPLETE for different numbers of cycles, and their MonBus streams diverged on channels whose commands matched exactly)

## Reproduction

`dv/tests/fub_g/test_stream_g_equiv.py::test_stream_g_equiv[gate-4-256-4-linear]` before the fix: every channel's
read and write commands identical on both sides, final states identical, MonBus packet counts 18 vs 26, 30 vs 32,
26 vs 22. The one-module scheduler's packet block emitted on every cycle spent in CH_FETCH_DESC and CH_COMPLETE.
The FSM leaves FETCH_DESC after one cycle, so DESC_START was one packet; but a LAST descriptor holds COMPLETE
until every write has committed (`w_write_complete`), and for those cycles the scheduler sent its DESC_COMPLETE
(or IRQ) packet again and again -- one IRQ event per cycle of the commit wait. Correct is one packet per visit:
one DESC_START on entry to FETCH_DESC, one DESC_COMPLETE / IRQ on entry to COMPLETE (the in-place prefetch advance
already sends its one). CH_ERROR had its one-shot guard already.

## Fix

`scheduler_core`: `w_enter_fetch` (FETCH_DESC and not the cycle before) and `w_enter_complete` gate the two packet
cases. The product regression's MonBus checks pass with one packet per descriptor; the stream-g equivalence bench
then matches packet for packet.
