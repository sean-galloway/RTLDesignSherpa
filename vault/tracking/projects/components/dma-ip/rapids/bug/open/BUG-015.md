# BUG-015: snk_data_path_axis stress test stalls the shared AXIS ingress past the BFM timeout

**Priority:** P3
**Status:** open
**Owner:** sean galloway

## Reproduction

`dv/tests/macro/test_snk_data_path_axis_test.py::test_stress[func-8-64-256-8-4096-default]`,
`REG_LEVEL=FUNC SEED=13275`. The test passes, but the log shows the AXIS
master's `TIMEOUT waiting for ready after 1000 cycles` 114 times on the HEAD
scheduler (2026-10-18, before rapids TASK-028) and 76 times with the
descriptor skid; with `send_descriptor(wait_taken=True)` a channel's
scheduler also fails to take a paced descriptor within 20000 cycles (32 and
20 times). Seen at gate, func and full across configurations.

## Expected

With each descriptor taken by its scheduler before its data is sent, the
head AXIS beat always belongs to a channel holding its packet record, so
the ingress should accept it within the transfer time; no ready timeout and
no dropped beat.

## Notes

- The stress test picks random channels and random addresses up to 4 MB
  into each channel's window, waits 10-30 cycles between operations, and
  scores only whether a send raised; it checks no memory, so the BFM's
  dropped beats never fail it.
- Not caused by the skid (same seed, fewer stalls with it). The other three
  paced tests (`axis_reception`, `axi_write_operations`, end-to-end) show
  no stall at any level.
- First step: find what holds `s_axis_tready` low at the first timeout
  (sink SRAM full, the write engine stalled on a random address, or the
  packet record), then make the test check memory.
