# BUG-016: axi4_dwidth_to_axil4_wr_chain narrow_within_wide gets 1 narrow beat per wide beat at 128 to 64

**Date:** 2026-10-28
**Priority:** P2
**Severity:** MEDIUM
**Status:** OPEN
**Owner:** unassigned
**Found by:** the converters area full regression (`make run-all-full-parallel`, 2026-10-28, run as the gate for the `peakrdl_to_cmdrsp` INPUT_REG change, whose eight-source filelist does not overlap this chain's)
**Affects:** `axi4_dwidth_to_axil4_wr_chain` at S_AXI = 128 b, M_AXIL = 64 b (ratio 2:1), every level; the 64 to 32 and 128 to 32 cells pass

## Summary

`test_axi4_dwidth_to_axil4_wr_chain[params6|7|8]` (gate, func, full of the
128-to-64 point) fail the chain TB's `test_narrow_within_wide` scenario
(`dv/tbclasses/axi4_dwidth_to_axil4_wr_chain_tb.py`), deterministically
across the suite's three reruns:

```
narrow_within_wide: got 1 narrow beats, expected 4
narrow_within_wide: got 4 narrow beats, expected 16
narrow_within_wide: got 8 narrow beats, expected 32
```

The scenario drives bursts of 1, 4 and 8 wide beats one size narrower than
the wide bus and expects `ratio * 2 = 4` narrow AXIL beats per wide beat;
the chain emits one per wide beat. Whether the expectation or the chain is
wrong at this ratio is the question: the same scenario passes at 4:1
(128 to 32) and at 2:1 from 64 (64 to 32), so it is the 128 b, narrow-size
64 b case specifically. Correct looks like either the TB's count or one
narrow beat per narrow-size wide beat, with the address increment matching
the narrower size; state which.
