# BUG-012: a split write loses its B when the slave answers its first part before its final AW issues

**Date:** 2026-10-20
**Priority:** P1
**Severity:** HIGH
**Status:** CLOSED 2026-10-20 (fixed; verified in sim and on the board)
**Owner:** unassigned
**Found by:** the Genesys 2 bridge stress model's third-party build (`projects/fpga-systems/Genesys2/bridge-stress/build-xil`) on the board, located with Ocelus
**Affects:** `axi4_dwidth_converter_wr` in DOWNSIZE mode, so every bridge write path that narrows a burst too long for one master burst

## Summary

A slave burst that needs more than 256 narrow beats is split into several
master bursts. The B fold (BUG-008's per-burst CAM) pushed the burst's entry
when its FINAL master AW issued, and only that final AW waited for a free CAM
slot. A B response matching no CAM entry is accepted (`q_bready` is 1 off a
match) and dropped.

So with the CAM full, the first part goes out -- its AW is not gated -- its W
completes, and a slave may answer it while the final AW still waits for a
slot. That B matches nothing and is dropped. The entry is pushed later
expecting every part's B, gets one fewer, and the slave burst never gets its
B: the write hangs, and with it every later same-ID write (the bridge holds a
same-ID write to another slave behind it).

## Seen

| Where | What happened | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Genesys 2 build-xil, cpu (64-bit) into mem1 (32-bit cdc slave three masters share), `narrow` profile | about 1 run in 50 of 4000 writes: one cpu write never got its B; no slave held it (mem1 `out_now` 0); Ocelus: the cpu port's monitor timed out on that write (`AXI_TIMEOUT_RESP id=0x32 addr=0x1617b9b8`, mem1) and on the next same-ID AW, no slave-side monitor timed out | board 2026-10-20 15:05, `reports/board/bridge_bs_xil_20261020_150531.csv` |

The CAM fills when the cpu holds 16 writes outstanding behind the shared
slave's queue; the first part of a split burst then gets through while its
final AW waits.

## Reproduction

`dv/tests/test_axi4_dwidth_converter_wr.py::test_axi4_dwidth_converter_wr_early_b`
(64 -> 32, the bridge's path): 16 single-beat writes fill the CAM with their
Bs held, then one 129-beat write (two master bursts); a slave-like responder
answers each master burst's B at its WLAST and holds the fillers' Bs until a
split part has been answered or the master W stalls. Before the fix: "split
part 1 answered with the CAM full", then the split write never gets its B.

## Fix

The burst's CAM entry is pushed at its FIRST master AW issue, carrying the
number of master bursts it splits into (ceil(narrow beats / 256)), and the
first AW -- not the final one -- waits for a free slot. Every B the burst will
get finds its entry. The open-burst tracking registers are gone.

## Verified

- `test_axi4_dwidth_converter_wr_early_b`: fails on the old RTL (the window
  opens and the split write hangs), passes on the fix.
- converters suite at func: 184 passed, 1 xfail (pre-existing
  axi3_to_axi4_wr_wid_mismatch).
- bridge suite at func: 512 passed.
- the board, build-xil `narrow`: 300/300 with zero Ocelus records, where the
  old RTL lost a write about once in 50 runs
  (`reports/board/bridge_bs_xil_20261020_162240.csv`).

## Also found

Every pytest wrapper in `test_axi4_dwidth_converter_wr.py` runs all four
cocotb tests (the `TESTCASE` env var is ignored), so each test's mode flags
leak into the next; not fixed here.
