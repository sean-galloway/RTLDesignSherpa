# BUG-002: the observers' compression statistics nets are declared and read into the CSRs but never driven

**Status:** OPEN 2026-10-24
**Priority:** P2
**Filed from:** stream TASK-020 phase 6 (the Vivado synthesis log of the Genesys 2 STREAM builds; the same seven "does not have driver" lines appear in every stream_perf build, STREAM and stream-g alike)

## Reproduction

Any Vivado synthesis of a design holding `axi4_intf_slave_observer` or `axi4_intf_master_observer`
(`projects/components/utility-ip/misc/rtl/`) reports, for both modules:

```
WARNING: Net w_comp_stat_tier0 in module/entity axi4_intf_slave_observer does not have driver.
WARNING: Net w_comp_stat_cam_miss in module/entity axi4_intf_slave_observer does not have driver.
WARNING: Net w_comp_stat_event_data_ovf in module/entity axi4_intf_slave_observer does not have driver.
```

`w_comp_stat_tier0`, `w_comp_stat_cam_miss` and `w_comp_stat_event_data_ovf` are declared
(`axi4_intf_slave_observer.sv:1076-1079`) and feed `OBS_COMP_STAT0.TIER0`, `OBS_COMP_STAT1.CAM_MISS` and
`OBS_COMP_STAT1.OVERFLOW` (`:1684-1686`), and no statement in either observer, nor in anything they include,
assigns them -- `grep -rnE 'w_comp_stat_(tier0|cam_miss|event_data_ovf)\s*(<=|=)'` over `utility-ip/misc/rtl`
and `rtl/amba/ocelus` finds nothing. Verilator reads them as 0 and Vivado ties them to 0, so the three
compression statistics CSRs read 0 on every build, including `USE_COMPRESSION = 1`, where the compressor's
counters exist and are the obvious intended source. Correct is either the compressor's counters wired to these
nets, or the nets and the three CSR fields removed if the statistics were retired.

## Notes

Found by the undriven-net scan `make bitstream` now runs after every build (`make/fpga_flow.mk`), added when
the same warning class hid a dead register path in the first stream-g bitstream (stream TASK-020). An
undriven net in synthesis is one the simulator drove: a lint that elaborates does not flag it (Verilator
reports nothing here), so the Vivado log is the only place it shows.
