# BUG-021: a monitored port reports false ORPHAN errors once it has more than 8 transactions outstanding

**Date:** 2026-10-20
**Priority:** P2
**Severity:** MEDIUM
**Status:** CLOSED 2026-10-20 (fixed; verified in sim and on the board)
**Owner:** unassigned
**Found by:** the Genesys 2 bridge stress model's third-party build with Ocelus (`projects/fpga-systems/Genesys2/bridge-stress/build-xil`, `bridge_bs_xil_mon`), on the board
**Affects:** every generated `_mon` variant: each port's monitor wrapper (`axi4_{master,slave}_{rd,wr}_monlite`)

## Summary

The generator instantiates the per-port monitor wrappers without
`MAX_TRANSACTIONS`, so every monitor's table holds the wrapper default, 8
transactions. Every port's outstanding limit is its `max_outstanding`
(default 16; `MAX_OUTSTANDING (16)` in the same adapters). By the lite
monitor's own contract (`axi_monitor_lite.sv` header), a command that finds no
free slot is counted, not tracked, and its beats then surface as
`AXI_ERR_DATA_ORPHAN` / `AXI_ERR_RESP_ORPHAN` errors. So any port that runs
more than 8 deep floods the error FIFO with orphans for traffic that is
correct, and real errors are lost in them.

## Seen

| Port | What happened | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| `bridge_bs_xil_mon` cpu (64-bit, IW 6), read | a `clean` run (16 reads outstanding, `out max 16`) filled the error FIFO past the host's 64-record drain with `PktTypeError AXI_ERR_DATA_ORPHAN` on `cpu.rd`; the run's data checked clean | board 2026-10-20 14:11, `reports/board/bridge_bs_xil_20261020_141138.csv` |

The bridge's own `_mon_monitor` suites never reach 9 outstanding on one port,
so they pass.

## Expected

A port's monitor tracks every transaction the port can have outstanding:
`MAX_TRANSACTIONS` = that port's `max_outstanding` (master side: the master
adapter's; slave side: the slave's). Orphans are reported only for beats with
no matching command.

## Also

The same wrappers take `ACLK_MHZ` (default 100) and set their
frequency-invariant tick's LUT to `CFI_MIN = CFI_MAX = ACLK_MHZ`, so
`cfg_freq_sel` selects nothing: a monitor's microsecond is 100 cycles of its
own clock. The bridge passes no `ACLK_MHZ`, so a port on a clock other than
100 MHz times out on a scaled clock (a lane monitor on a 75 MHz cdc slave:
1.33 us per tick). Not a correctness bug; recorded so a timeout reads right.

## Fix (2026-10-20)

The generator passes `MAX_TRANSACTIONS` = the port's `max_outstanding` to
every monitor wrapper (`Axi4TimingWrapper(max_transactions=...)`, from the
master and slave adapter generators and the lane-slave monitor). Regenerated
output changed by exactly the 113 new parameter lines.

## Verified

- generator unit test `test_monitor_table_covers_port_outstanding`: every
  monitor in the mcdc mon fixture carries its port's limit.
- bridge suite at func: 512 passed.
- build-xil harness sim `clean`: false orphans on the old bridge, none after.
- the board: `clean` 3/3 and `narrow` 300/300 with zero Ocelus records.
