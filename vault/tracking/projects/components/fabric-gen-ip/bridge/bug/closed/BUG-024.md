# BUG-024: crossed-W deadlock between a CDC (lane) slave and a slot slave

**Date:** 2026-10-21
**Priority:** P1
**Severity:** HIGH
**Status:** CLOSED 2026-10-22 (fixed; verified in sim and on the board)
**Owner:** unassigned
**Found by:** the Genesys 2 bridge stress model on the board (`build-axi4`, `reorder` seed 1, `reports/board/bridge_bs_axi4_20261021_202856.csv`), the first full-AXI run after bridge BUG-018/023; reproduced exactly in simulation
**Affects:** a generated crossbar with a `cdc = true` slave that two or more masters share (bridge W9 lanes) and at least one other slave those masters write

## Summary

A shared CDC slave is not arbitrated in the crossbar: each master's AW
enters its own lane into the slave's crossing, and `bridge_cdc_slave_arb`
picks on the slave's clock and orders W by that pick. A slot slave orders W
by its pick on `aclk`. The crossed-W hold (`_aw_gate`) only made a master's
AW wait while ANOTHER REQUEST SLOT held one of its AWs; an AW already in a
lane is invisible to it. So master A's older AW could sit in the lane slave's
crossing while A's newer AW was picked first at the slot slave, and the lane
slave, on its own clock, could pick master B ahead of A. The two slaves then
wait on each other's W heads.

## Measured (board and sim agree exactly)

`reorder` seed 1: every write master stops at ~100 bursts (cpu.wr issued 104
completed 97; dma 62/52; acc 126/111; io 99/92), reads complete, no data
errors. Snapshot at the hang (sim): mem0's W owner head = acc, acc's W
destination head = mem3, io's = mem3; mem3 (the CDC slave) takes W only on
the cpu/dma lanes (`lane_wready` 0011) while acc/io hold W (`lane_wvalid`
1100); cpu's and dma's W heads = mem0. With the old host limit
(converted paths aligned full-width INCR only) the same seed passes, so the
BUG-018 converter's extra AWs (and the full W destination FIFO they cause)
are what makes the window likely; the cycle itself is the fabric's.

## Fix

`crossbar_generator.py`: per master path and lane slave, a live-W count --
up at that path's AW handshake toward the lane slave, down at its WLAST into
the lane. `_aw_gate` holds a master's AWs to every other slave while any of
its counts is non-zero (any width path). Once a master's W for a lane burst
is in the lane, its W stream no longer depends on the lane slave's order, so
no master can be stuck in front of it. Regression:
`bin/tests/test_generator_pkg.py::test_lane_slave_live_w_holds_other_aws`
(fails on the old generator). Sim: `reorder` seed 1 x 400 PASS (hung at
~100 before), `bursts` seed 1 x 800 PASS.
