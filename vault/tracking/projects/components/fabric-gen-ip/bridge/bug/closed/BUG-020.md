# BUG-020: a write to a slave that waits for WVALID before AWREADY deadlocks the bridge

**Date:** 2026-10-20
**Priority:** P1
**Severity:** HIGH
**Status:** CLOSED 2026-10-20 (fixed; verified in sim and on the board)
**Owner:** unassigned
**Found by:** the Genesys 2 bridge stress model's third-party build (`projects/fpga-systems/Genesys2/bridge-stress/build-xil`), on the board
**Affects:** every generated crossbar; any slave port whose slave may hold AWREADY low until it sees WVALID

## Summary

AXI A3.3.1: a slave may wait for AWVALID, WVALID or both before asserting
AWREADY, and a master must not wait for AWREADY (or WREADY) before asserting
WVALID. The generated crossbar routes a write's W beats through a per-master
destination FIFO (`<m>_wdest_*` in `<bridge>_xbar.sv`) that is pushed only
when the slave port **accepts** the AW. Until then no slave's WVALID can rise.
So towards each slave port the bridge, acting as master, waits for AWREADY
before WVALID; a slave that waits for WVALID first never raises AWREADY, and
the write -- and with it the master's whole W path -- hangs for good.

Every slave in the repo's own suites raises AWREADY without W (the stress
slaves, the framework's slave BFMs), so no sim and no earlier board build
could see it.

## Seen

| Slave | What happened | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| AXI Central DMA register slave (`axi_cdma` v4.1, AXI4-Lite) on `bridge_bs_cfg`'s `ext1` window | the first register write never got its B; every later host access timed out (the configuration bridge stays wedged until reprogram). Reads of the same registers work | board 2026-10-20; access log ends `W 0x110018 ... False` |
| (not this bug) MIG 7-series DDR3 on `bridge_bs_xil`'s `mig` port | write sweeps hang after 78-468 bursts. Suspected at first; an ILA (2026-10-20) showed MIG's own controller raise `app_rd_data_valid` with no read issued anywhere and then hold `app_rdy` low for good -- a MIG / PHY fault on this board, recorded in the bridge-stress PRD, not a bridge defect | `reports/` ILA capture, bridge-stress PRD findings |

## Root cause (2026-10-20)

Two places released W only after the slave's AW handshake. Measured with a
stress-slave mode that does what the CDMA does (`w_first`: AWREADY only while
WVALID is up), on the bridge stress model's AXI4 harness in simulation: every
master's writes stopped after a handful of bursts, and a port-state dump at
the hang showed mem3 -- the clock-crossing slave -- with AWVALID up, waiting
for W, and no WVALID.

| Block | Defect | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| `bridge/rtl/bridge_cdc_slave_arb.sv` (a slave on its own clock that two or more masters share) | W followed an owner FIFO pushed only at the slave-side AW handshake: no owner, no WVALID | owner bypass, as the generated crossbar already had for in-clock slaves: with the FIFO empty the lane whose AW is being presented owns W (the grant is a flop, so no READY is involved); `own_ahead` keeps a burst whose WLAST went first from pushing a stale owner |
| `converters/rtl/axi4_to_axil4_wr.sv` (every AXI4-Lite, AXI5-Lite and WB4 slave port) | a single beat's AWREADY was the slave's own, and a burst beat's W went only with or after its AW handshake | every AW is registered; each AXI4-Lite beat completes when its AW and its W have both handshaked, in either order; WVALID never waits on AWREADY |

The in-clock crossbar was not affected: its owner bypass (bridge W3) already
let W ahead of the AW handshake for AXI slaves, and the AXI4-Lite / APB / WB4
shims took the bypass off because of the converter above.

## Verified

- AXI4 harness sim, `w_first` profile: deadlocked before, passes after.
- the stress pair test's `w_first` scenario (AXI4 / AXI3 / AXI5): the stress
  master never waits for AWREADY, and the scenario proves the gate held (no
  AW taken without a W beat offered, at least one AW waited for its W).
- converters `axi4_to_axil4_wr` and the dwidth chain: 11/11 at func, after
  their monitors stopped assuming AW-before-W (they paired a W only with an
  AW already seen and dropped any that came first).
- converters suite at func: 184 passed, 1 xfail (pre-existing); bridge
  suite at func: 512 passed (2026-10-20, after every later generator change
  too).
- the board, build-xil with the CDMA register slave directly on ext1 (the
  AW skid removed): `w_first` 20/20, the CDMA's register writes answered,
  `narrow` 300/300 (2026-10-20, `reports/board/bridge_bs_xil_20261020_171410.csv`,
  `..._162240.csv`).

## Correct

Towards a slave, WVALID must not depend on that slave's AWREADY. The W route
for a burst can be committed when the crossbar's AW arbitration grants it
(the destination is known then), not when the slave handshakes it; or each
slave port takes the AW into a stage of its own (a skid) so the slave's
handshake no longer gates the route.

## Workaround in build-xil (removed)

`rtl/bs_genesys2_xil_top.sv` carried a `gaxi_skid_buffer` on the AW channel
between the bridge and the DMA's register slave until the fix: the skid took
the AW at once, the route was pushed, W flowed. It is gone; the DMA's
register slave is on ext1 directly.

## Gate

Done: the stress slaves' `WR_CTRL.W_FIRST` holds AWREADY until WVALID, and
the `w_first` profile runs in the harness suite on every build and on the
board.
