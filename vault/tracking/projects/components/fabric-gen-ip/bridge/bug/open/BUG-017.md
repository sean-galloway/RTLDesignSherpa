# BUG-017: read bandwidth drops below the perf floors once R interleaving is on

**Date:** 2026-10-18
**Priority:** P1
**Severity:** HIGH
**Status:** open
**Owner:** Claude (bridge-updates session)
**Affects:** `dv/tests/test_bridge_perf_char.py`, every read case, on every fixture in `MATRIX`; writes are unaffected

## Summary

R interleaving landed on 2026-10-18 (PRD FR-9). It had three parts: the crossbar and
master-adapter R arbiters re-arbitrate every beat instead of holding
through RLAST; the read width converters are built `R_INTERLEAVE = 1`; and the
generated TBs' slave BFMs interleave R (`interleave_r`, RDS-DV 1.2.7,
`BRIDGE_R_INTERLEAVE=1` by default). In the first full regression afterwards,
the perf suite's read cases missed their floors. Every write case still meets
its floor.

Correct: read slave_rate at the floors it held before the change (0.970 for
the saturated cases), since interleaving should not cost read bandwidth.

## Measured (gate cells, slave_rate vs floor)

| Fixture | Case | Measured | Floor |
|---|---|---|---|
| bridge_2x2_rw | outstanding rd lat=16 n=4 | 0.803 | 0.970 |
| bridge_2x2_rw | outstanding rd lat=64 n=16 | 0.887 | 0.970 |
| bridge_2x2_rw | contention rd lat=32 out=8/1 | 0.918 | 0.970 |
| bridge_2x2_rw | fairness rd accept=inf out=8/2 | 0.883 | 0.970 |
| bridge_2x2_rw_lat | outstanding rd lat=64 n=8 | 0.627 | 0.776 |
| bridge_2x2_rw_cdc @13 | fairness rd accept=inf out=8/1 | 0.795 | 0.970 |
| bridge_2x2_rw_cdc @13 | outstanding rd lat=64 n=8 | 0.613 | 0.730 |
| bridge_4x4_rw | outstanding rd lat=64 n=8 | 0.654 | 0.748 |

Miss counts across all levels: outstanding rd 117, fairness rd 93, contention
rd 63, bursts rd 10. Writes: 0.

## First observations

- bridge_2x2_rw has no width converters, so `R_INTERLEAVE = 1` in the
  converter is not the whole cause.
- Two suspects remain. The first is the RTL: per-beat R re-arbitration in
  the crossbar or adapter path arbiter. The second is the test model: the
  interleaving slave BFM keeps at most two beats queued, refilled once per
  clock edge, which may leave bubbles on the R channel.

## Next step

Re-run the failing perf cells with `BRIDGE_R_INTERLEAVE=0` (BFM interleave
off, RTL unchanged). If they recover, the BFM is the cause. If not, the RTL is:
compare against the hold-through-RLAST crossbar generated into scratch.
