# BUG-040: BUG-039's consumer test fails at HEAD -- 2 of 800 events unaccounted

**Priority:** P2
**Status:** CLOSED 2026-10-16 (TB stimulus fix; DUT/monitor proven correct)
**Owner:** seang
**Filed:** 2026-10-16

`projects/components/utility-ip/misc/dv/tests/fub/test_axi4_intf_observer.py`
`consumer_sustained[0/1/5]` and `consumer_board_rate[5-10]` fail on a clean
detached checkout of HEAD (c39a9e1de era), e.g.

    duty=0 pace=0 offered=800 delivered=798 (99.8%) drop_reports=0 accounted=798
    duty=5 pace=0 offered=800 delivered=390 drop_reports=2 reported_drops=408 accounted=798

delivered + reported_drops falls 2 short of offered even with no consumer
backpressure, so 2 events are lost without being counted. BUG-039 closed
2026-10-09 with this test green; the likely candidate is 6c0bc51f7 ("monitors
tap the FUB side"). Not caused by amba TASK-046 (the gaxi queue change):
identical numbers with and without it.

## Root cause (named, 2026-10-16)

The 6c0bc51f7 FUB-side tap move is correct and exposed a STIMULUS defect.
Boundary-counter instrumentation of the failing duty=0 cell proved the
whole observation path conserves exactly:

    mon allocs (cmd accepted)     = [400, 400]   refused=0 dropped=0 orph=0
    mon completions (w_compl)     = [399, 399]   mon queue pushes = [399, 399]
    arbiter ingress accepted      = [399, 399]   group = 798   m_axil = 2394 beats

so the loss is INSIDE the monitor boundary and is not a drop/refusal/orphan
(every one of those is counted and was zero). Per-ID analysis pinned the
missing completions to arid=15 / awid=7 == the LAST read and LAST write
burst of the run. End-state probe: `rd_data_valid=1 rd_data_ready=0`,
`wr_resp_valid=1 wr_resp_ready=0` -- the final R/B beat parks forever in
the observer's registered tap path with no ready to complete it.

Mechanism: `AXI4IntfObserverTB.drive_read_burst/drive_write_burst` modelled
the master by blipping RREADY/BREADY high for exactly ONE cycle per beat.
The observer's R/B pass-through is a registered tap; a response beat's
core-side handshake can land one cycle after its source-side beat, so the
per-beat ready blip is one cycle short per burst. Every mid-run burst is
rescued by the NEXT burst's blips (observable in sim: each completion fires
~2 cycles after the next AR). The final burst of each channel has no
successor: its last beat parks, the monitor never sees data_hs/resp_hs for
it, and one completion per channel silently never exists. Pre-FUB-tap the
monitor sampled the bus side where the blips coincided by construction, so
the defect was masked. A real AXI master holds xREADY until transfer and
keeps accepting while a beat is presented -- the DUT and monitors behave
correctly against one.

## Fix

- `dv/tbclasses/axi4_intf_observer_tb.py`: RREADY is now held from before
  the first R beat through one trailing cycle after the last (BREADY
  likewise held one cycle past B). AXI-correct master modelling; the
  trailing cycle covers the registered tap's core-side handshake.
- `cocotb_test_observer_egress_ceiling`: reads and writes are now offered
  by two concurrent coroutines (independent channels). The sequential offer
  at beats=2 had only a thin margin over the 0.15 assert; the BUG-040 holds
  add fixed per-record cycles, and longer bursts REDUCE the record rate
  (one record per transaction regardless of beats). Concurrent offer
  restores a real limiter: measured egress 0.220 records/cycle.
- The consumer test keeps the BUG-040 boundary counters (taps/path/ids) as
  permanent diagnostics -- a future silent loss now localizes in one run.
