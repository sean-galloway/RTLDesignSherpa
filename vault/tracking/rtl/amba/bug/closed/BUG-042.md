# BUG-042: the 1wr TB checks IRQ accounting before the acknowledged-record pool drains

**Priority:** P3
**Status:** CLOSED 2026-11-21
**Owner:** TBD

## Expected

For every error record accepted and acknowledged, `monbus_group_core_1wr` emits
one `irq_out` pulse and increments `err_rec_count`. The shared TB must wait until
that accounting finishes before comparing both values with the stimulus count.

## Observed

Clean FULL `val/amba` failed only
`test_monbus_axi4_group_1wr[errors_only-256]`: 148 pulses and
`err_rec_count=148` for 150 records, despite all 450 beats being present in seven
acknowledged error bursts. The failure reproduced in isolation with `SEED=5527`.
Instrumentation showed `r_err_beats_acked=6` at the check: exactly two complete
records remained in the accounting pool. The RTL was not losing records.

## Cause and fix

B responses can arrive in bunches near capture completion. The core drains one
acknowledged record per cycle, but the TB waited a fixed 40 cycles after capture
and then checked immediately. The TB now waits, bounded at 1024 cycles, until
`err_rec_count` reaches the expected number before calling `check_irq`.

RED: the original TB fails the exact seed with 148/150 and six beats pending.
GREEN: the same seed passes after the wait. The AXI4 wrapper, AXIL wrapper, and
bare core then pass 24/24 combined FULL cells with `SEED=5527`.

This was a verification timing defect, not a production RTL change.
