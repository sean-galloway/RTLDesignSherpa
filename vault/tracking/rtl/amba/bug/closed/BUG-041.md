# BUG-041: latency histogram -- HIST_SAMPLE_LOST set on a full FIFO, and a full FIFO with a same-cycle pop dropped its command

**Priority:** P2
**Status:** CLOSED 2026-10-23 (RTL fix, unit + observer tests, mutation-checked)
**Owner:** seang
**Filed:** 2026-10-23

Two defects in `rtl/amba/shared/axi_perf_latency_hist.sv`, one of them
surfacing through the observers'
`OBS_STICKY.HIST_SAMPLE_LOST`
(`projects/components/utility-ip/misc/rtl/axi4_intf_{master,slave}_observer.sv`).

## 1. The sticky reported losses that did not happen

The observers set `HIST_SAMPLE_LOST` on `o_cmd_block`. That signal is
"the timestamp FIFO for the channel on `cmd_id` is full". It is a
would-block signal: it has no `cmd_valid` term and is high whenever a
channel sits at its limit. A one-channel master running exactly
`HIST_MAX_OUTSTANDING` deep keeps it high with nothing lost. The Genesys 2
fabric benchmark read the flag set on one-channel W2 runs whose bins and
totals were exact on the board (2026-10-23).

## 2. A command meeting a same-channel pop at a full FIFO was dropped

`w_push = w_cmd_hs && (r_cnt[w_ch_cmd] < MAX_OUTSTANDING)` did not credit a
completion retiring on the same channel in that cycle. `o_cmd_block` did
credit it, so it said "not blocked" while the push failed. The command was
never timestamped and was missing from `o_hist_total`. Its completion later
popped another command's timestamp, misattributing the latencies after it.
Nothing flagged it.

## Fix

- One condition, `w_full_ch = r_cnt >= MAX && !(same-channel pop)`, now drives
  three signals:
  - `w_push = w_cmd_hs && !w_full_ch`. The tail write is safe beside the
    head read, because the head timestamp is read combinationally.
  - `o_cmd_block = w_full_ch`, unchanged in meaning.
  - a new port `o_sample_lost = w_cmd_hs && w_full_ch && !i_freeze`, which
    pulses on the loss itself.
- Both observers build the sticky on `o_sample_lost`.
- `stream_core` and `char_engine_block` gain an explicit empty
  `.o_sample_lost()`.

## Tests

- `val/amba/test_axi_perf_latency_hist.py`, all 4 builds (rd/wr x 8/1
  channels):
  - `latency_hist_full_is_not_loss_test`: full channel held, and a command
    offered without ready; `o_sample_lost` must stay 0.
  - `latency_hist_full_push_with_pop_test`: depth rounds of command plus
    same-channel completion at a full FIFO; all 2 x depth counted, none
    lost, no latency under 32 cycles.
  - The overflow test now also requires exactly one `o_sample_lost` pulse
    per dropped command.
- `projects/components/utility-ip/misc/dv/tests/fub/test_axi4_intf_observer.py`
  `*_observer_hist_sample_lost` (master and slave): exactly at the limit, the
  sticky is clear and the total is 8; two past the limit, it is set.
- Mutations, each restored and checked with `cmp`:
  - Old `w_push`: `full_push_with_pop` fails in all 4 builds.
  - `o_sample_lost = w_full_ch`: `full_is_not_loss` fails in all 4 builds,
    and the observer test fails on both observers.
