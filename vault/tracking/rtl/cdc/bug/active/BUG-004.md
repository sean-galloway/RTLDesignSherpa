# BUG-004: glitch_free_n_dff_arn carries no ASYNC_REG attribute

> Source: found during the mem-ctrl FPGA microarchitecture review
> (memory-controllers ISSUE-001), 2026-11-05, reading the synchronizer every
> `gaxi_fifo_async` pointer crossing uses.

**Priority:** P2 -- every async FIFO in the repo crosses its Gray pointers
through this module; without the attribute Vivado may place the two stages in
different slices (or extract them into an SRL), the MTBF argument for the
2-flop synchronizer does not hold, and `report_cdc` cannot classify the
crossing as safe.
**Status:** active 2026-11-06 -- attribute added; val/cdc gate 37/37; board confirmation (report_cdc) pending.
**Owner:** TBD
**Where:** `rtl/cdc/glitch_free_n_dff_arn.sv:339` (`r_q_array` declaration).

## Observed

The header (`:225-245`) documents the attribute and gives the exact line to
add: `(* ASYNC_REG = "TRUE", SHREG_EXTRACT = "NO" *)` on `r_q_array`. The
declaration at `:339` has no attribute. The two handshake synchronizers in the
same directory do carry it (`cdc_2_phase_handshake.sv:121-129`,
`cdc_4_phase_handshake.sv:104-110`), so this is the one odd module out.

Consumers: `gaxi_fifo_async` (`rtl/cdc`), `mc_dfi_cdc`'s async branch
(memory-controllers BUG-002), `cdc_synchronizer` in `mc_training_shell`, the
APB CDC in the char harnesses.

## Expected

Both flops of every synchronizer stage pair placed together and kept as flops,
with the CDC report recognising the crossing. On Xilinx that is the
`ASYNC_REG` attribute on the array; `SHREG_EXTRACT = "NO"` stops SRL inference
of the shift.

## Fix shape

Add the attribute on `r_q_array` (one line, `:339`). Then rebuild one A7 image
and confirm with `report_cdc` that the Gray-pointer crossings move from
"unknown" to "safe", and check the synth log for no `SRL` extraction on the
synchronizer. No functional change; sim is unaffected.

## Fix (2026-11-06)

`(* ASYNC_REG = "TRUE", SHREG_EXTRACT = "NO" *)` on `r_q_array`
(`rtl/cdc/glitch_free_n_dff_arn.sv`). Simulation unaffected: `val/cdc`
gate 37 / 37. Closing needs one Artix-7 build with `report_cdc` showing the
Gray-pointer crossings classified and no SRL on the synchronizer.
