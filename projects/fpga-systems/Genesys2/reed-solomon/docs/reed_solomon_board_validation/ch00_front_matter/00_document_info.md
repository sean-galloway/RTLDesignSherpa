## Scope

This book covers the RS(252,236) t=8 codec as exercised on the Digilent
Genesys 2 board. It describes the harness, the campaign methodology, the
2026-10-05 battery, the erasure boundary finding, and the completed
million-block soak.

## Provenance

All timing and campaign results in this book come from `stable/MANIFEST.md`,
`stable/results/2026-10-05_battery/`, and
`stable/results/2026-10-05_soak/`. The board used for the runs is
Digilent Genesys 2 serial `200300B818A0` (Kintex-7 `xc7k325t-2`), programmed
and measured with Vivado 2025.1.
