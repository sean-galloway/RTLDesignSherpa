# DFI v4.0 Master Interface, and the v3.1 Delta

## The revision decision

andesite targets **DFI 4.0** `§TBC(TASK-005)` — the DDR4-era revision per
the bootstrap spec §2. The reasoning is short and on the record: scoria's "deliberately
unimplemented" table is almost entirely the DDR4/LPDDR4 surface of its v3.1 —
`dfi_act_n*`, `dfi_bg*`, CA parity and `dfi_alert_n*`, DBI, CA training.
andesite's job list *is* that table. Targeting v3.1 and hand-adding the DDR4
pins would rebuild the boundary scoria already drew; targeting 4.0 makes the
boundary match the work.

**Citation discipline for this chapter.** The DFI 4.0 `§TBC(TASK-005)`
specification is not on disk — andesite TASK-005 acquires and studies it — so
every clause reference in this chapter is suffixed `§TBC(TASK-005)`. Signal
*names* are public and stated plainly; clause *numbers* are
to-be-confirmed, and no invented number appears anywhere below.

## What does NOT change

**The datapath is inherited entirely.** One async-FIFO CDC, bubble-free, the
unit of transfer a DFI word, at the inherited 1:4 frequency ratio. The
phase-multiplied buses carry: the `_pN` generalization scoria adopted from
v3.1 still stands, and the 1:4 ratio remains a defined mode of the newer
revision (DFI 4.0 §TBC(TASK-005)).

**The leveling request/acknowledge scheme carries.** scoria's per-chip-select
leveling handshake (`dfi_phylvl_req_cs_n` / `dfi_phylvl_ack_cs_n` with
`dfi_phy_wrlvl_cs_n` selecting the training) transfers to 4.0 unchanged in
shape (DFI 4.0 §TBC(TASK-005)) — which matters because andesite's two new
training blocks are built directly on it.

**The init and error surface carries.** `dfi_init`, `dfi_error` and
`dfi_error_info` are scoria's already; the DDR4 CA-parity detail that
`dfi_error_info` was widened to carry is now used rather than ignored.

**`dfi_reset_n` is inherited.** scoria drives it from the init sequencer and
presents it at the DFI boundary under its DFI name; andesite keeps that
convention (Ch 3.2).

**The low-power requests stay exposed and unacknowledged-by-default.**
`dfi_lp_ctrl_req` / `dfi_lp_data_req` remain presented, with the inherited
rule that an unacknowledged request times out and reports — never blocks.
The dormant-pair condition of Chapter 3.1 governs whether anything ever
consumes them.

## What changes and matters: the 3.1 → 4.0 delta

| Signal / behavior (DFI 4.0, clauses §TBC(TASK-005)) | What scoria drove at v3.1 | What andesite drives | Citation |
|---|---|---|---|
| `dfi_act_n*` — the fifth command pin | absent; ACT was an encoded command | driven from `dfi_cmd_formatter` (MODIFIED); ACT_n plus RAS/CAS/WE forms the activate | §TBC(TASK-005) |
| `dfi_bg*` — bank-group address | absent; no bank groups | driven with BG0/BG1 during activate, from the `addr_mapper` decode | §TBC(TASK-005) |
| `dfi_cid*` — chip ID | absent | not driven at `NUM_RANKS = 1`; presented constant, like scoria's data-phase chip selects | §TBC(TASK-005) |
| gear-down handshake | n/a — not in v3.1 | `init_sequencer` drives entry after init; the CA bus then runs at half rate (Ch 3.2) | §TBC(TASK-005) |
| `dfi_parity_in` — CA parity | `dfi_error`/`dfi_error_info` carried the indication; parity encodings ignored | `dfi_cmd_formatter` generates parity; the counting machinery's hw/fw split is an open question (Ch 6) | §TBC(TASK-005) |
| `dfi_alert_n*` — parity/training alert | absent | input to a NEW `alert_n` handling submodule at the boundary; surfaces through `dfi_error` | §TBC(TASK-005) |
| `dfi_wrdata_dbi*` / `dfi_rddata_dbi*` — Data Bus Inversion | absent | driven, MR5-programmed (read and/or write DBI), through the MODIFIED aligner and serializer | §TBC(TASK-005) |
| `dfi_phy_rdlvl_cs_n`, `dfi_lvl_pattern`, `dfi_lvl_periodic` — read leveling and pattern | present in v3.1, unused (write leveling only) | driven by `rdlvl_ifc` for MPR-based read leveling (Ch 3.3) | §TBC(TASK-005) |
| `dfi_calvl_*`, `dfi_ca_capture`, `dfi_phy_calvl_cs_n` — CA training | absent | driven by `ca_train_ifc` for LPDDR4 CA/WDQ training (Ch 3.3, 3.6) | §TBC(TASK-005) |
| frequency-ratio options beyond 1:4 | n/a at this design point | not exercised; deferred until a board PHY requires one | §TBC(TASK-005) |

: Table 4.1: The 3.1 → 4.0 delta — what scoria left unimplemented is andesite's work list

**The trap to record, carried from scoria's chapter.** A naive signal-name
diff between revisions reports phase-indexed names as appearing and
disappearing with each revision's notation. They don't; the `_pN` form is a
generalization, and a 1:4 implementation keeps four concrete phases. An
implementer who trusts the diff rewrites a working, verified datapath for no
reason — so the trap stays on the record here, one revision later.

## What andesite does NOT implement

Named, so the absence is a decision:

| Group | Why not |
|---|---|
| The DDR5-oriented features the DFI 4.x revision family also carries (ch00's not-in-scope line) | No DDR5 in this controller; named here so the boundary is a decision §TBC(TASK-005) |
| Frequency ratios beyond the inherited 1:4 | No board PHY to consume them; the datapath's phase structure transfers at the gear it was verified at §TBC(TASK-005) |
| `dfi_lp_*` consumption | The dormant-pair condition of Ch 3.1; the requests stay presented per the timeout rule above |

Targeting the newer revision does not mean implementing all of it — the same
sentence scoria's chapter closed on, one revision later.

## The MAS seam

This chapter names the boundary; the MAS ch03 pins it — every signal above
appears there as a pin-level row (name, direction, clock, reset value,
source block), and the clause column there carries the same
`§TBC(TASK-005)` discipline for DFI 4.0 §TBC(TASK-005). The DV framework's
DFI signal catalog — the check that, for scoria, caught a missing required
signal by name — runs against this table when the BFM exists (andesite
TASK-005).
