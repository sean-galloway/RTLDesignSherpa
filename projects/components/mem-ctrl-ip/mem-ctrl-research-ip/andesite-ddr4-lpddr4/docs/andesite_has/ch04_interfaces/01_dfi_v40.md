# DFI v4.0 Master Interface, and the v3.1 Delta

## The revision decision

andesite targets **DFI 4.0** (§1.0 Overview, §2.2.3 DFI Signals) — the
DDR4-era revision per the bootstrap spec §2. The reasoning is short and on the
record: scoria's "deliberately unimplemented" table is almost entirely the
DDR4/LPDDR4 surface of its v3.1 — `dfi_act_n*`, `dfi_bg*`, CA parity and
`dfi_alert_n*`, DBI, CA training. andesite's job list *is* that table.
Targeting v3.1 and hand-adding the DDR4 pins would rebuild the boundary scoria
already drew; targeting 4.0 makes the boundary match the work.

**Citation discipline for this chapter.** The DFI 4.0 specification is now on
disk (`/mnt/data/github/dfi-specs/DDR_PHY_Interface_Specification_v4_0.pdf`)
and was studied on 2026-10-04 as andesite TASK-005. Clause references below are
real DFI 4.0 section numbers; any remaining `§TBC(TASK-005)` marks a claim the
study could not confirm. Signal *names* are public and stated plainly.

## What does NOT change

**The datapath is inherited entirely.** One async-FIFO CDC, bubble-free, the
unit of transfer a DFI word, at the inherited 1:4 frequency ratio. The
phase-multiplied buses carry: the `_pN` generalization scoria adopted from
v3.1 still stands, and the 1:4 ratio remains a defined mode of the newer
revision (§4.9 Frequency Ratios Across the DFI).

**The init and error surface carries.** `dfi_init_start` / `dfi_init_complete`,
`dfi_error` and `dfi_error_info` are scoria's already (§3.5.1 Initialization,
§3.8 Error Interface); the DDR4 CA-parity detail that `dfi_error_info` was
widened to carry is now used rather than ignored.

**`dfi_reset_n` is inherited.** scoria drives it from the init sequencer and
presents it at the DFI boundary under its DFI name; andesite keeps that
convention (§3.1 Control Interface, Table 3; Ch 3.2).

**The low-power requests stay exposed and unacknowledged-by-default.**
`dfi_lp_ctrl_req` / `dfi_lp_data_req` remain presented, with the inherited
rule that an unacknowledged request times out and reports — never blocks.
The dormant-pair condition of Chapter 3.1 governs whether anything ever
consumes them (§3.7 Low Power Control Interface).

## What changes and matters: the 3.1 → 4.0 delta

| Signal / behavior (DFI 4.0) | What scoria drove at v3.1 | What andesite drives | Citation |
|---|---|---|---|
| `dfi_act_n*` — the fifth command pin | absent; ACT was an encoded command | driven from `dfi_cmd_formatter` (MODIFIED); ACT_n plus RAS/CAS/WE forms the activate | §3.1 Control Interface |
| `dfi_bg*` — bank-group address | absent; no bank groups | driven with BG0/BG1 during activate, from the `addr_mapper` decode | §3.1 Control Interface |
| `dfi_cid*` — chip ID | absent | not driven at `NUM_RANKS = 1`; presented constant, like scoria's data-phase chip selects | §3.1 Control Interface, §4.3 3DS Stack Support |
| `dfi_geardown_en` — gear-down mode | n/a — not in v3.1 | `init_sequencer` drives entry after init; the CA bus then runs at half rate (§3.13, Ch 3.2) | §3.13 Geardown Mode |
| `dfi_parity_in` — CA parity | `dfi_error`/`dfi_error_info` carried the indication; parity encodings ignored | `dfi_cmd_formatter` generates parity; the counting machinery's hw/fw split is an open question (Ch 6) | §3.5.7 CRC and CA Parity |
| `dfi_alert_n*` — parity/training alert | absent | input to a NEW `alert_n` handling submodule at the boundary; surfaces through `dfi_error` | §3.5.7 CRC and CA Parity |
| `dfi_wrdata_mask` (write DBI) / `dfi_rddata_dbi` — Data Bus Inversion | absent | write DBI reuses `dfi_wrdata_mask` when DBI is enabled; read DBI is `dfi_rddata_dbi`; MR5-programmed through the MODIFIED aligner and serializer | §3.2.1 Write Data Mask/Write DBI, §3.3.1 Read DBI, §4.4 Data Bus Inversion |
| `dfi_phy_rdlvl_cs`, `dfi_lvl_pattern`, `dfi_lvl_periodic` — read leveling and pattern | present in v3.1 as `dfi_phy_rdlvl_cs_n`, unused (write leveling only) | driven by `rdlvl_ifc` for MPR-based read leveling (Ch 3.3); v4.0 drops the `_n` suffix on training chip selects | §3.6 DFI Training Interface, §3.6.1 Read Training Operation, §3.6.6 dfi_lvl_pattern Encoding, §3.6.7 Periodic Training Flag |
| `dfi_calvl_*`, `dfi_calvl_capture`, `dfi_phy_calvl_cs` — CA training | absent | driven by `ca_train_ifc` for LPDDR4 CA/WDQ training (Ch 3.3, 3.6); v4.0 drops the `_n` suffix on `dfi_phy_calvl_cs` and adds LPDDR4 VREF signals (`dfi_calvl_data`/`done`/`result`/`strobe`) | §3.6.4 CA Training Operation, §4.12.7 CA Training |
| frequency-ratio options beyond 1:4 | n/a at this design point | not exercised; deferred until a board PHY requires one | §4.9 Frequency Ratios Across the DFI |

**Study correction (2026-10-04, andesite TASK-005).** The v3.1 per-CS
leveling handshake `dfi_phylvl_req_cs_n` / `dfi_phylvl_ack_cs_n` does *not*
transfer unchanged into DFI 4.0. The 4.0 spec removes those PHY-requested
training signals and replaces them with PHY Independent Mode (§3.6.8
PHY-Initiated Training in DFI Training Mode). All training chip-select
signals are also renamed from `*_cs_n` to `*_cs` in 4.0 (e.g.
`dfi_phy_rdlvl_cs_n` → `dfi_phy_rdlvl_cs`, `dfi_phy_calvl_cs_n` →
`dfi_phy_calvl_cs`). andesite's training interface must use the v4.0 names;
any reuse of v3.1-style `phylvl_*` handshakes is an andesite-specific
convenience, not a DFI 4.0 signal.

A second correction: there is no `dfi_wrdata_dbi` pin in DFI 4.0. Write DBI
is carried on the existing `dfi_wrdata_mask` bus when DBI is enabled and
`phydbi_mode = 0` (§3.2.1). Read DBI has the dedicated `dfi_rddata_dbi`
signal (§3.3.1).

**The trap to record, carried from scoria's chapter.** A naive signal-name
diff between revisions reports phase-indexed names as appearing and
disappearing with each revision's notation. They don't; the `_pN` form is a
generalization, and a 1:4 implementation keeps four concrete phases. An
implementer who trusts the diff rewrites a working, verified datapath for no
reason — so the trap stays on the record here, one revision later.

## What andesite does NOT implement

Named, so the absence is a decision:

| Group | Why not |
|---|---|
| The DDR5-oriented features the DFI 4.x revision family also carries (ch00's not-in-scope line) | No DDR5 in this controller; named here so the boundary is a decision (§2.2.1 Global Features, §2.2.2 Memory Topology-Specific Features) |
| Frequency ratios beyond the inherited 1:4 | No board PHY to consume them; the datapath's phase structure transfers at the gear it was verified at (§4.9 Frequency Ratios Across the DFI) |
| `dfi_lp_*` consumption | The dormant-pair condition of Ch 3.1; the requests stay presented per the timeout rule above (§3.7 Low Power Control Interface, §4.13 Low Power Control Handshaking) |

: Table 4.2: Deliberately unimplemented at the 4.0 boundary

Targeting the newer revision does not mean implementing all of it — the same
sentence scoria's chapter closed on, one revision later.

## The MAS seam

This chapter names the boundary; the MAS ch03 pins it — every signal above
appears there as a pin-level row (name, direction, clock, reset value,
source block), and the clause column there carries the same DFI 4.0 section
references. The DV framework's DFI signal catalog — the check that, for
scoria, caught a missing required signal by name — runs against this table
when the BFM exists (andesite TASK-005).
