Notes

  • Formula: MTBF = exp(C2 * tMET_total) / (C1 * f_clk * f_data). C1 in seconds, C2 in s-1, all times in seconds.
  • Resolution budget: tMET_total = t0 + (N - 1) * r, where r = (1 / f_clk) - tCO - tSU. Each added flop buys roughly one extra clock period of metastability resolution time.
  • Convention follows Clifford E. Cummings' SNUG synchronizer papers; this app is a teaching aid, not a foundry sign-off model.
  • Technology presets are illustrative literature ballparks. Real C1/C2 vary widely with process, voltage, temperature, and flop design.
  • The "flipping point" is the stage count where MTBF crosses the mission time. At 1 GHz with the 28 nm preset, N = 2 fails a one-year mission but N = 3 passes it.
