Notes

  • Margin convention: the almost-full side reserves ceil(f_wr / f_rd) * (N + 1) items; the almost-empty side reserves ceil(f_rd / f_wr) * (N + 1) items. These are worst-case integer bounds for N-stage pointer synchronizer uncertainty.
  • Bubble tolerance is taken from each end of the FIFO. A 10% bubble on a depth-32 FIFO removes 3 slots from the top and 3 from the bottom.
  • PASS requires AF_threshold > AE_threshold. If the depth is too small for the chosen margins and bubble, the design must use a larger FIFO, fewer synchronizer stages, or a smaller bubble.
  • Pointer encoding split: power-of-2 depths use Gray pointers (USE_JOHNSON=0); even non-power-of-2 depths use Johnson pointers (USE_JOHNSON=1). Illegal combinations fail at elaboration.
  • Clock drift is not modeled: enter worst-case numbers directly (fastest writer, slowest reader for AF; slowest writer, fastest reader for AE).
